## 📜 Daily Spell Preparation

> 🎯 **You may check up to 17 total spells per long rest** (Wizard Level 12 + INT 20).  
> Cantrips are always known and don’t count toward the limit.

## 🪄 Cantrips *(Always prepared — auto-scaled for level 12)*
- [x] **Minor Illusion** *(Illusion Cantrip)*  
  📏 **Range:** 30 ft 🕒 **Casting Time:** 1 action ⏳ **Duration:** 1 minute (Concentration not required)  
  🔮 Create a **sound** or **image** that fits in a 5-ft cube. The image is static and silent; the sound can range from a whisper to a scream.  
  🎭 Used to distract, disguise, or create false cover. Perfect for misdirection or creative problem solving. Creatures may investigate (INT check) to disbelieve.

- [x] **Prestidigitation** *(Transmutation Cantrip)*  
  📏 **Range:** 10 ft 🕒 **Casting Time:** 1 action ⏳ **Duration:** Up to 1 hour  
  ✨ Create harmless sensory effects. Excellent for flair, subtle signals, or RP flavor. Such as: flavor food, clean or soil an object, light/snuff small flame, chill/warm an item. 

- [x] **Dancing Lights** *(Evocation Cantrip)*  
  📏 **Range:** 120 ft 🕒 **Casting Time:** 1 action ⏳ **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute  
  💡 Create 4 torch-sized lights (dim light in 10 ft each) that float, move, or form into a glowing humanoid shape.  
  🌀 Lights can move up to 60 ft as a **bonus action**.  

- [x] **Ray of Frost** *(Evocation Cantrip)*  
  📏 **Range:** 60 ft 🕒 **Casting Time:** 1 action  
  ❄️ Fire a beam of cold at one creature:  
  - **Hit:** 3d8 cold damage (Level 11+)  
  - **Effect:** Target’s speed is reduced by 10 ft until your next turn.  

- [x] **Fire Bolt** *(Evocation Cantrip)*  
  📏 **Range:** 120 ft 🕒 **Casting Time:** 1 action  
  🔥 Launch a mote of fire at a creature or flammable object:  
  - **Hit:** 3d10 fire damage (Level 11+)  
  - **Special:** Ignites flammable objects not worn or carried.  

- [x] **Mage Hand** *(Conjuration Cantrip)*  
  📏 **Range:** 30 ft 🕒 **Casting Time:** 1 action ⏳ **Duration:** 1 minute  
  🤲 Summon a spectral hand that can: Open doors, pick up or drop items, interact with simple objects.

- [x] **Message** *(Transmutation Cantrip)*  
  📏 **Range:** 120 ft 🕒 **Casting Time:** 1 action ⏳ **Duration:** 1 round  
  💬 Whisper a short message to a creature within range, even through objects or walls (as long as there's a path).  
  - Target can whisper back immediately. Ideal for stealth missions, split parties, or hidden talk mid-combat.
