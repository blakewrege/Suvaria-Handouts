## Complimentary Homicide Directions

*One murder. Three sensible questions. No refunds.*

- Ask **Miss Whiskerby** what crossed Frankquill's calendar.
- Ask **Tilda Kettle** what crossed Frankquill's tea tray.
- Ask **Mallow & Sons Import Storage** why harmless dirt requires so much public money.
- Ask **Grand Librarian Horace Folio** why the **Grand Bureau of Civic Contentment** borrowed codes from a wartime office. It was neither grand nor content.
- **Andruw Junction looks guilty. That is either useful or the point.**

**Courtesy warning:** Frankquill was not killed for what he knew. He was killed because he was about to prove it.

*—Compliments of Deep Snoat. The coaster is complimentary. Follow-up secrets are not.*
