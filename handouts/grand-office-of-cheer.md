# Recovered Archive Summary
## The Civic Contentment Bureau and the Grand Office of Civic Cheer

*Prepared by Grand Librarian Horace Folio from records that officially did not exist, twice.*

## The Modern Office

- **Name:** Grand Bureau of Civic Contentment
- **Authorized lifespan:** Eleven days
- **Payments approved:** Six
- **Programs funded:** Public Morale Kitchens, Green Route Couriers, Civic Nutrition Review, **Open Ring Petition Hospitality**, greenhouse supplies, and emergency food wagons
- **Approving authority:** A bundled authorization bearing the Vice-Ringleader's seal
- **Current status:** Dissolved before its rented furniture arrived

The seal appears authentic, but its spacing follows a government style abandoned twenty-five years ago. This suggests the seal was copied from an older document rather than applied by Vice-Ringleader Andruw Junction.

## The Open Ring Connection

Four vendors paid by the modern Bureau also supplied free meals, temporary workers, and greenhouse freight to the campaign that pressured Leonia to pass the **Open Ring Accord**. Older manifests connect two of those vendors to hospitality events supporting Mycoterra's rejected provincial petition five years ago.

This does **not** prove that every supporter or signature was false. It proves that someone repeatedly used a genuine political movement as cover for the same food, courier, and cultivation network.

Frankquill circled the matching route codes and wrote:

> “Same soup. New office. More voters.”

## The Wartime Office

Its seal and account codes belonged originally to the similarly named **Grand Office of Civic Cheer, Nutrition, and Labor Harmony**, an agency of the former **Confederated Cap States of Mycoterra**.

The office:

- Distributed compulsory work rations
- Managed agricultural labor assignments
- Tracked escaped slaves
- Censored casualty reports
- Used calming mushroom cultures in its so-called **One-Up Work Rations**
- Contracted **House Calhoun of Forks Hollow** to conduct numbered ration trials

Its surviving slogan reads:

> **ONE PEOPLE. ONE PURPOSE. ONE-UP.**
>
> *A cheerful worker is a productive worker. A productive worker has nothing to fear.*

## Frankquill's Request

Shortly before his death, High Juggler Benny Frankquill requested:

- The original wartime charter
- The ration formula
- Modern vendors using **GOC**, **GREEN ROUTE**, or **KEEP DAMP** routing codes
- Every payment involving **Mallow & Sons Import Storage**

He left one handwritten note:

> “The numbers are not merely growing roots. They are growing old roots.”
