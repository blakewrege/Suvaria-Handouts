# Bracelet of Arena Memento

*Wondrous item, common; does not require attunement*

> **Current owners:** Amerilis, Bells, Black Chao, Bobbin, Caracas, and Krunk

The percentage meters, emergency eliminations, and other tournament enchantments faded after the first Trial. Each competitor's bracelet retained one small piece of arena magic—and a legally binding reminder that Armskirk accepts no responsibility for heroic landings.

## Double Jump

**Once per long rest**, while airborne, you may use your **reaction** to propel yourself up to **20 feet** horizontally, vertically, or in a combination of both.

The jump must follow an unobstructed path. If it does not carry you to a surface capable of supporting you, you begin falling normally after the movement ends. The bracelet does not otherwise prevent falling damage.

## Retired Arena Functions

Outside the Super Clash Bros arena, the bracelet does **not** provide a percentage meter, Telekinetic Shove, automatic ledge recovery, damage conversion, or protection from death. Those features belonged to the arena enchantment, not the souvenir.
