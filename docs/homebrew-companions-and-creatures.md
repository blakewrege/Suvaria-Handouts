> ##### Time to Drop Knowledge
> The blink dog is an intelligent canine that takes its name from its ability to blink in and out of existence, a talent it uses to aid its attacks and to avoid harm. Blink dogs harbor a long-standing hatred for displacer beasts and attack them on sight.
**Culture:** Blink dogs tend to live in closely knit packs consisting of related individuals. Much like wolves, these packs are generally comprised of a family unit--a mother, a father, and their children. Unlike wolves, it is not uncommon for a pack to also include extended family members (grandparents, aunts, uncles) and the adult children of the pack, which are welcome to stay and expand the pack by bringing home a mate.
**Language:** Blink dogs have their own language, a mixture of barks, yaps, whines, and growls that can transmit complex information.
**Combat:** Blink dogs hunt in packs, teleporting in a seemingly random fashion until they surround their prey, allowing some of them to take advantage of flanking.
**Maturation Rate:** Blink dogs will mature into an adult at about 10 months(at which point ask me for a new sheet).
**Warranted Suspicion:** Blink dogs tend to be highly suspicious of other races, as the wicked and unscrupulous have long sought their fortune by stealing blink dog pups with the intention of "taming" and "training" them and then selling them for outrageous amounts of coin. Given that blink dogs are sapient creatures the equal of any human, dwarf, or elf, this practice is understandably viewed by blink dogs as kidnapping and slavery.
**Curious Companions:** Although most blink dogs keep strictly to their own kind, some eccentric individuals chose to attach themselves to roving "packs" of adventurers, spurred on by curiosity.
