Legacy Magic Items¶

Historical catalog: This imported list includes current, retired, sold, and unclear-ownership equipment. Standalone player item handouts are canonical where linked.

Maul of Maelstroms (Caracus)¶

Maul, uncommon (requires attunement)

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The bonus damage is lightning damage. Once per long rest you can shout a battle cry and slam an enemy dealing an extra 1d10 thunder damage. They must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked back 10ft and prone. You feel a slight shock whenever you pick it up, the weapon seems to vibrate with energy

Ring of the Gladiators (Krunk)¶

Wondrous item, common

Explosive applause every time you crit to attack or deliver a killing blow to an enemy. Add 1d6 to your next attack roll. Laugh track if the wielder crit-fails on an attack roll. Subtract 1d6 to your next attack roll.

Gilly's Bag of Beans (Caracus?)¶

Wondrous item, uncommon

|#| Color | Effect | |:----:||:----:|:-------------| |1.| Yellow | Ipicac | |2.| Purple | Eagle | |3.| Green | Bear | |4.| Red | Bull | |5.| Blue | Cat | |6.| Grey | Owl | |7.| Brown | Fox | |8.| Black | Unknown | As a bonus action you may eat or give a bean. Most of these beans Enhance Ability but several do other things. Make a Intelligence check (DC 12) to pick the correct bean. On a fail, roll a d8 and use that to select the color. The duration is 10 minutes. Party currently has 20 beans. Gilly warns only to eat one bean per person before taking a rest.

Ring of Charm (Bells)¶

Wondrous item, uncommon

3 charges per day. You may cast Charm Person (DC 15) but you must shake the targets hand(with your ring hand) in order to do so. The target does not learn you tried to charm it on the first usage regardless of success or fail.

Eyes of the Eagle (Ella)¶

Wondrous item, uncommon

While wearing them, you have advantage on Perception and Investigation checks that rely on sight. In conditions of clear visibility, you can make out details of even extremely distant creatures and objects as small as 2 feet across.

Boots of Elvenkind (Vex)¶

Wondrous item, uncommon

While you wear these boots, your steps make no sound, regardless of the surface you are moving across. You also have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks that rely on moving silently.

Veil of Shadows (Krunk)¶

Wondrous item, rare

A large black amulet also known as Shield of the Void. Once per day you can use a Bonus Action or a Reaction to speak this amulet's Command Word to create an aura that lightly obscures and gives resistance to all damage types until your next turn to everything in a 10 ft radius around you.

Sword of Screams (Krunk)¶

Longsword, uncommon

When pulled from it’s scabbard everyone within 60ft of it is deafened for 1d4 turns. It has the same stats as a Longsword and is magical. While weilding this weapon you may ask DM the to play selected screamo or metal music.

Helm of Invisibility (Ella)¶

Wondrous item, uncommon

This helmet that makes the wearer's head invisible. You may use this helm once per day to give yourself Advantage on an Intimidation or Deception check.

Talking Skull (Bells)¶

Wondrous item, uncommon

This skull has forgotten its name and seems to be constantly eyeing you. Once per day you can animate the skull, lighting up its eyes with a pale blue light. Roll a d6 and a d100. While animated it speaks with you and will answer a question to the best of its ability if you correctly guess a riddle.

Garland of Garlic (Caracas)¶

Net, uncommon

A magical garland of garlic laced with silver barbs. On hit, it wraps around the vampirie's neck and prevents it from using its bite action. Cannot be removed or destroyed by a vampire regardless of shape changing. Has the same properties as a Net except it does not restrain.

Bone Breaker (Caracus)¶

Mace, uncommon (requires attunement)

A large spiked mace that says “Bone Breaker, do not use!” hastily written across it. 1d6 bludgeoning, +1 piercing, +1 to hit, is Magical - Break Bones (Recharge 5-6): Melee Attack, on hit the target must succeed a Constitution Save(DC 14) or have their AC reduced by 1 and speed reduced by 10ft until healed. - Hunger for Blood: At the start of combat the wielder must make a Wisdom Save(DC 14). On fail, you must dash and Reckless Attack(Barbarian) an enemy within melee range of Bone Breaker. - Bloodied Rage: While the wielder is bloodied in combat this weapon deals an extra 1d4 bludgeoning damage. At the start of his turn the weilder must make a Constitution Save(DC 14) to avoid attacking the nearest creature (random if more than one adjacent).

Chilling Halberd (Krunk)¶

Halberd, uncommon (requires attunement)

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon and can apply the Call of the Grave affect when you hit. - Call of the Grave (Recharge 6): Add 1d6 Necrotic Damage, a ghostly skeletal hand attaches to the target and they can’t regain hit points until the start of your next turn. Until then, the hand clings to the target. If you hit an undead target, it also has disadvantage on its next attack roll against you until the start of your next turn.

Shadow Dagger (SOLD)¶

Dagger, rare (requires attunement)

You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. - Returning: Shadow Dagger returns to the wielder’s hand immediately after it is used to make a ranged attack. - Backstabber: Roll an addtional 1d4 damage when the target is flatfooted. - Create Undead Minion (Long Rest): Once per long rest you may spend an action to stab the Shadow Dagger into a fresh corpse. The corpse becomes an undead minion and obeys your commands until it is destroyed or you use this ability again. (high CR creatures will be reduced to one)

Demon Mask (SOLD)¶

Wondrous item, uncommon

This terrifying mask is crafted in the visage of a leering demon and grants a +1 item bonus to Intimidation checks. - Leering Gaze (Long Rest): The mask casts the fear spell with a DC of 14(or spell bonus) on a single target that can see it. This doesn't require concentration of the wearer.

Shield of Friendship (???)¶

Wondrous Sheild, uncommon

As a bonus action, you may send your shield to another creature (1) up to 30ft away, giving them +2 AC until the start of your next turn. Until the sheild returns you do not benifit from it's bonus. If your hands are full at the start of your turn the sheild appears at your feet or in a holster you are wearing.

Horschopper (???)¶

Wondrous Guisarme, uncommon

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. This magical makeshift +1 Guisarme can be weilded with one hand but deals 1d6 instead of 1d10 when attacking someone 10ft away

Necklace of lightning bolts (Strawberry)¶

Wondrous Guisarme, uncommon

Once per turn, you can use an action to detach a bead. When you do a 3rd-level Lightning Bolt spell (save DC 14) is created in the direction of your choice.

You can detach multiple beads, or even the whole necklace, as one action. When you do so, increase the level of the Lightning Bolt by 1 for each bead beyond the first. You may use this while wildshaped if the creature has enough manual dexterity.

Kinesis Module (Ella)¶

Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)

  • Kinesis Grab: As an action, you can cast Mage Hand. The spectral hand created by this spell is invisible.

  • Kinesis Launch (Recharge after a Short Rest): Once per short rest, you can cast Catapult at the 4th level without using a spell slot (6d8). The damage dealt by this ability can be modified by the item you choose to throw.

The Kinesis Module, inspired by Dead Space, grants the wielder telekinetic abilities. This magical item is perfect for tactically minded characters who wish to manipulate their environment and use objects as weapons in creative ways.

Minor Items¶

Orc War Helm: Reduce damage on a critical hit taken by 1d10. -1 to Perception Savage Battle Axe: Add 1d4 to damage when attacking an unarmored opponent Mind Flayer Whip: +1 damage nonmagical whip Shadar-Kai Spear: (SOLD) Standard spear that grants +1 to Intimidation Staff of the Crab: Once per day you can cast Dominate Beast on a creature or swarm with a swim speed that is CR 1 or lower. Spiked Shield: (SOLD) When you use the shove action and succeed you may use this sheild to deal 1d4 + Strength piercing damage.

Elixir of Enhanced Eloquence (Bells)¶

Wondrous potion, rare (requires attunement)

A potion that, when consumed, gives the drinker a silver tongue. It gives a permanent +1 to Charisma skill checks (not impacted . It also allows the user to cast "Comprehend Languages" once per day without using a spell slot.

Gloves of the Archival Knowledge (Caracus)¶

Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)

You have Advantage on History checks to decipher an objects origin. Once per long rest, the wearer can cast the "Mending" spell on a broken historical document or magical artifact (may require a DC check).

Historian’s Horologe (Krunk)¶

Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)

Description: This magical pocket watch allows its owner to transport back to a previously set position once per day.
