The Snoat Sheet¶

A Professional's Guide to Publicly Humiliating Your Future Colleagues¶

Your Ring¶

  • Grand Ring Authority — Wilsun Fyske: Trade, industry, and the solemn belief that every public problem deserves a privately owned solution.
  • United Assembly — A. Hamil: Competent, prepared, respectable, and capable of turning a sentence into a committee.
  • Veiled Concord — Darkveil: Secretive populists endorsed by the Glow Cloud. All hail.
  • Borb's Buns — Borb: Beloved food, powerful feelings, uncertain sanitation.

Questions They Are Likely to Throw at You¶

  1. Why should Leonia trust outsiders with leadership?
  2. Who pays for your promises?
  3. What will you do when guild profit and public need collide?
  4. How will you keep Armskirk, Torath, and Corvos pulling in the same direction?
  5. Which disaster in your history should the Audience pretend not to have heard about?

Across the Other Ring¶

The Bardy Bunch sing their answers. The Wizened & Confused forget theirs. The Guild of Mild Convenience want standardized business hours. The Protagonists Ascendant all claim to be the chosen one.

Watch the Redcap Resolutes. They are the only joke that is not laughing.

Final Advice¶

The best argument does not always win. The best moment usually does.

Once during the debate, your team may quote or act upon this sheet before a social roll to gain advantage—or use one verified scandal to impose disadvantage upon an opponent's response. One use total. Scandal is a spice, not a meal.

Deep Snoat accepts no responsibility for elections, reprisals, thrown pastries, or facts deployed without adequate theatrical timing.
