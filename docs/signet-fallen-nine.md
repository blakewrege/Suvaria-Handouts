# Signet of the Fallen Nine

<div class="portal-note"><strong>Archive audit notice:</strong> This handout was released before Session 077 and may contain older mechanics, names, links, or lore phrasing that still needs review. The table's latest ruling is authoritative.</div>

*Wondrous item (ring), very rare (requires attunement by a non-good spellcaster; cursed)*

### Provenance
This signet is a lesser work of shadow—neither one of the Nine nor a Ring of Power in its own right, but **forged to harmonize with them**. Smiths loyal to the Dark Lord cast it in the fumes of Orodruin’s fires, quenching the band in the chill of Morgul water and setting a **black glass gem** cut from the same volcanic obsidian used to edge Morgul blades. In ages past, **Khamûl the Easterling** bore rings that answered the will of the One; this signet **remembers** that cadence. It is a **tuning fork for wraithdom**, designed to steady death magic, reveal the half-seen, and—over days—pull its wearer across the veil.

### Appearance
- A lean, iron-dark band with **hammered facets** that never reflect warmth.  
- The inner rim bears hair-fine **gouges like teeth marks**; under moonlight they **crawl into legible sigils** 
- The stone is a lens of night. Stare long enough and you’ll notice **pin-prick lights moving far beneath**, as if watching you back.

---

## The Temptation of Power
The ring grows stronger the **longer it is worn continuously** (attuned and on your person).

- **Stage I — Dormant:** Immediately on attunement.  
- **Stage II — Stirring:** After **3 consecutive days** worn.  
- **Stage III — Ascendant:** After **7 consecutive days** worn.

If you remove the ring for **24 hours** or receive *remove curse*, drop **one stage** (min I).

### Favor of the Eye (Doing Sauron’s Bidding)
When you **complete a task Sauron explicitly commands** through the ring (DM adjudicates), you may claim his **Favor** within 24 hours:
- **Instantly set the ring to Stage III (Ascendant) for 24 hours.**  
- **Boon:** Until the next day, the undead you control have **advantage on their first attack** each combat and you may cast Danse Macabre and Shadow of Moil without using a spell slot.

---

## Shared Properties (All Stages)

**Shadow Anchor (1/long rest).** *Reaction* when you take damage: you may **move up to 10 feet**, then become **Shadowform** until the start of your next turn— you can move through creatures/objects as difficult terrain and have **resistance to all damage except radiant and force**. While in Shadowform you **can’t cast spells, make attacks, or take reactions**. If you end inside an object, take **1d10 force** and are shunted to the nearest space.

**Sacred Bane.** You have **vulnerability to radiant**. While on holy ground (e.g., *hallow*), you have **disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks**.

---

## Stage I — Dormant
- **Necromantic Aptitude I.** **+1** to **spell attack rolls and damage** with necromancy. Undead you control gain **+1 to attack and damage rolls**.  
- **Veil-Piercer I.** Your **darkvision increases by 30 ft.**, and you can **see through magical darkness**.  
- **The Voice Whispers.** **Sauron can whisper to you**—short, unmistakable messages the DM may deliver at any time (no action, any distance/plane).

---

## Stage II — Stirring
*(Includes Stage I benefits unless replaced.)*
- **Necromantic Aptitude II.** **+2** to **spell attack rolls and damage** with necromancy. Undead you control gain **+2 to attack and damage rolls**.  
- **Veil-Piercer II.** While in **dim light or darkness**, you have **see invisibility**.  
- **Grave Tether.** When a creature drops to 0 HP from a necromancy spell you cast, gain **temporary HP equal to your level/2** (once per turn, rounded up).  
- **The Eye Turns.** **Sauron knows your general location** (direction and within a few miles) while you wear the ring.  
- **Taint of Shadow.** You have **disadvantage on Persuasion checks** against good-aligned creatures and exude a faint, unsettling chill.

---

## Stage III — Ascendant
*(Includes Stage II benefits unless replaced.)*
- **Necromantic Aptitude III.** **+3** to **spell attack rolls and damage** with necromancy. Undead you control gain **+3 to attack and damage rolls**.  
- **Veil-Piercer III.** **Constant see invisibility**; in dim light/darkness you also gain **blindsight 10 ft.**  
- **Wraith’s Command (1/short rest).** *Reaction:* When a **creature** dies within **60 ft.**, you may raise it as a **Wight** under your control for **10 minutes**. It acts immediately after your turn, **can’t create spawn**, and counts against any control limits. When the duration ends, it crumbles to ash.  
- **Sunlight Sensitivity.** In direct sunlight, you have **disadvantage on attack rolls** and on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.  
- **The Hand Commands (Geas of the Nine).** After completing a long rest while wearing the ring, make a **DC 21 Wisdom saving throw**. On a failure, you cannot remove the ring and you are affected by **_geas_** (no further save) until the next day with a command delivered through the ring. Every hour you **directly disobey** the command, you take **2d10 psychic damage**, and the DM may impose **disadvantage on saves vs. charm and fear** until your next dawn. The effect ends early if you complete the command, or the ring is targeted by *remove curse* cast with a **7th-level slot** or higher.
