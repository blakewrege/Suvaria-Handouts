# Kara Vesh, Gauntlet of the Coiled Throne — Rules Review

<div class="portal-note"><strong>Archive audit notice:</strong> This handout was released before Session 077 and may contain older mechanics, names, links, or lore phrasing that still needs review. The table's latest ruling is authoritative.</div>


> **Formerly recorded as:** Black Gauntlet. **Status uncertain:** Excalibur may have destroyed Kara Vesh. It is not listed as current equipment until its fate—and the final mechanics—are confirmed.

*Wondrous item, rare and evil; requires attunement*

Black metal ribbons clamp around the hand and forearm, terminating in a blood-red stone set into the palm. The gauntlet was built as an instrument of domination: it understands protection chiefly as preserving its owner long enough to hurt someone else.

## The Hand of a False God

Yuan-ti tablets name the device **Kara Vesh**, or **Hand of the Coiled Throne**. It belonged to a dynasty of serpent god-kings who ruled through spectacle, terror, and stolen magic. Their priests claimed every flash of force and impossible command was divine power. Their engineers were forbidden from using the phrase “wearable arcane control system” anywhere near the worshippers.

The dynasty did not invent Kara Vesh. They stole its design from a vanished civilization, replaced its original power source with alchemically treated serpent blood, and added a neural interface that responds to domination as readily as thought. The red palm-stone reads intent, directs kinetic force, projects a brief shield, reaches into a victim's nerves, and commands compatible Yuan-ti doors, transit circles, constructs, and relic machinery.

Kara Vesh does not care about ancestry. It recognizes **evil** as command authority. A wielder willing to treat others as tools is, by its standards, properly credentialed.

## Legacy Protection — Preserved

These are the gauntlet's older rules, translated from the former character record:

- The wearer gains **+1 AC**.
- Against ranged attacks, the gauntlet instead provides **+2 AC**.
- The gauntlet may be used with a shield. While doing so, the wearer takes a **−2 penalty to Dexterity checks, Dexterity saving throws, and initiative** as the gauntlet locks the arm into a rigid defensive posture.

The old sheet recorded this as base AC 18 becoming **AC 19**, **AC 20 against ranged attacks**, or **AC 21 with a shield**, alongside “DEX −2.”

## Proposed Expanded Powers — Not Final

The gauntlet has **3 charges** and uses **save DC 16**.

### The Price of Power

- Kara Vesh regains all expended charges at dusk **only while attuned to an evil-aligned creature**. Otherwise, it regains no charges.
- A non-evil attuned wearer may **submit to the Coiled Throne** during a long rest. At the end of the rest, the wearer willingly changes their alignment to evil and Kara Vesh immediately regains all expended charges.
- This submission is a moral choice, not a removable condition. *Remove curse*, *lesser restoration*, and *greater restoration* do not reverse it. Changing back requires genuine atonement, redemption, or another story-level transformation determined with the DM.

Kara Vesh calls this arrangement voluntary. The fine print calls it **eternal strategic alignment**.

- **Ribbon Pulse — 1 charge, Action.** One creature within 30 feet makes a Strength save. On a failure, it takes `2d8` force damage and is pushed 15 feet directly away. On a success, it takes half damage and is not pushed.
- **Serpent Ward — 1 charge, Reaction.** When a ranged attack hits the wearer, the gauntlet adds **+5 AC against that attack only**, possibly causing it to miss. This does not stack with the gauntlet's ranged AC bonus; use whichever bonus is higher.
- **Neural Lash — 2 charges, Action.** One creature within 30 feet makes a Wisdom save. On a failure, it takes `3d6` psychic damage, cannot take reactions, and is frightened of the wearer until the end of the wearer's next turn. On a success, it takes half damage and suffers no other effect.
- **Tyrant's Touch — no charge, Action.** Manipulate one unattended object or unlocked mechanical control within 30 feet as though using *mage hand*. It cannot attack, pick locks, disarm traps, or lift more than 10 pounds.
- **Curse — System Lord's Pride.** Whenever a creature succeeds on a saving throw against the gauntlet, the wearer must succeed on a **DC 20 Wisdom save** or become fixated on punishing that defiance. Until the end of the wearer's next turn, they have disadvantage on attacks against creatures other than that target. The gauntlet continually whispers that mercy is merely poor management.
