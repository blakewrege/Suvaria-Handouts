Snake Blood Alley Delicacy Crawl¶

Twelve Dishes. Several Waivers. One Commemorative Spoon.¶

Have your card marked at any five stalls to earn an official I ATE SOMETHING IN SHEXUE XIANG spoon. The spoon is decorative and should not be used to test whether a dish is watching you.

Stall Featured Dish Price Tourist-Safe Description
Red Fang Broth Counter Snake-Blood Iron Soup 2 gp Ginger, dark herbs, and a metallic bite. Visitors mostly sweat.
Red Fang Broth Counter “Wake the Dead” Sour Soup 5 gp Vinegar, pepper, numbing root, and an irresponsible name.
Kun's Impossible Grill Ankheg Shell Crisps 1 gp Chili-dusted shell flakes crunchy enough to threaten weak teeth.
Kun's Impossible Grill Owlbear Rib Skewer 4 gp Huge, smoky, messy, and served with an apologetic napkin.
Lantern Vitality Tonics Snake Wine Shot 3 gp Strong liquor from a jar containing a snake of disputed employment.
Lantern Vitality Tonics Moon Antler Cordial 8 gp Sweet romantic courage. Mostly sugar; the courage is self-service.
Crooktail Pickle Jars Pickled Cockatrice Egg 4 gp Sharp, briny, gray-green, and texturally confrontational.
Crooktail Pickle Jars Old City Pickle Cup 5 cp Cheap vegetables in sour brine. Suspiciously normal and actually good.
Yu's Fire Bowl Shrine Monk's Fire Noodles 1 gp Hot enough to produce either enlightenment or paperwork.
Yu's Fire Bowl Shrine Trial by Pepper Dumplings 2 gp Six dumplings. One has chosen violence.
Quiet Ladle Night Cart Night Worker's Soup 5 cp Warm broth for tired couriers, porters, and people avoiding questions.
Huan's Dare Skewers Mystery Monster Ends 5 sp The mystery is the flavor. The waiver is the garnish.

Crawl Rules¶

  1. “Not poisonous” is a statement of confidence, not certification.
  2. Do not challenge Sister Pepper Yu unless tomorrow is optional.
  3. Photographs with the rotating eye jar cost 2 cp. Feeding it costs considerably more.
  4. Anyone claiming the tarrasque is genuine must provide the rest of the tarrasque.
  5. Lost tourists should follow the steam toward the loudest argument.

SHE XUE XIANG: Come hungry. Leave legendary. Digest privately.
