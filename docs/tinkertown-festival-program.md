# **Detailed Festival Events**

Below are expanded descriptions for Events 2–8, complete with **skill checks**, **funny/punny moments**, and **scene-setting** vignettes. Tinkertown’s colorful citizens all play a part, and you’ll find plenty of ways to engage your **Level 11** party in DnD 5e.

---

## 2. **Talent Show: “Rap & Wrap” Battle**

**Location**: A makeshift stage near **Brews and Blues**. Garland and giant candy canes decorate the platform, and a modest crowd of gnomes and dwarves eagerly awaits the show.

### **Scene Setting**

> *Paper streamers float on the evening breeze, and holiday lights cast dancing patterns on the stage. A tiny gnome in peppermint-striped pants steps up to the mic, adjusting his candy-cane microphone.*  
> **“Step right up, fold that paper tight,  
> spit your rhymes—show your holiday might!**  
> We’re about to rap while we wrap,  
> If you flop, watch your gifts unwrap!”*  

### **Mechanics & Skill Checks**
- Contestants must **rap** (Performance check, CHA-based) **and** **wrap** (Sleight of Hand or simple DEX-based) simultaneously.  
- Base **DC 14** for moderate success.  
- **DC 18+** yields a big crowd reaction and a small prize (e.g., a **Potion of MistleToe** which makes your breath smell like peppermint for 24 hours, or a handful of special cookies from **Munster Mart**).

#### **Rap Lines Examples**  
1.  
   > “I’m a gift-wrappin’ wizard, yo you betta believe,  
   > Twistin’ bows, droppin’ flows, got a trick up my sleeve.  
   > I’ll box this present tight like a gnome in a crate,  
   > Keep your eyes on me, ‘cuz I’m the illest in the state!”  

2.  
   > “Tape, tape, tape—gonna stick it in place,  
   > Ribbon so bright, it lights up the space.  
   > My bars so cold, I’m chillin’ the beat,  
   > While my paper corners sharper than a blacksmith’s heat!”  

> *A Tinkertown local, wearing a tinsel chain, waves the crowd to chant along, “Wrap it, rap it, wrap it, rap it!”*

## 3. **Igloo Building Contest**

**Location**: A fenced-off snowy yard just past **Francis’s Furniture**. Huge blocks of packed snow are piled high, along with **gnomish ice saws** that hum with faint mechanical energy.

### **Scene Setting**

> *Chunks of fresh snow glisten in the winter sun. Gnomes in thick mittens dart between snow piles, shaping frosty bricks with weirdly advanced ice saws. The air resonates with crisp frost and echoing laughter.*  

### **Mechanics & Skill Checks**
- **Strength (Athletics) DC 15** to carve or haul large snow blocks.  
- **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) DC 15** to fashion precise snow bricks or decorate edges.  
- **Arcana or Tinker’s Tools (DC 15)** for magical details (e.g., color-shifting icicles or functional snow doors).

#### **Notable Competitors**
- **Gnomer Simpson** attempts to help. He frequently ends up napping inside half-built igloos. *“Mmm… donut Igloo.”*
- **Miss Gnomer** of the Tinkertown Times snaps pictures, threatening to print embarrassing fails on the front page.

**Winning Prize**:  
- A satirical “**Igloo’d to Perfection**” ribbon—shaped like an upside-down ice cube.  
- Some **frozen cheddar bites** from **Munster Mart** as a comedic snack.

## 4. **Audience Participation Musical feat. Vocal Vance**

**Location**: A cozy corner stage behind **Dresselmeyer’s Emporium**.

### **Vocal Vance: The “Weird Al”-Style Accordion Singer**
- He’s a flamboyant gnome with an accordion bedazzled in LED lights.  
- Sings parodies of popular Tinkertown tunes—“*Teeny Cog Rhapsody*,” “*Like a Gnome Stone*,” etc.

### **Scene Setting**

> *A hush falls as a spotlight illuminates Vance, decked in polka-dotted robes. The clack of his accordion resounds, bouncing off the glistening cogs overhead.*  
> **“Allow me to polka your fancy!”** he cries, before launching into an outrageous parody ballad about misfired inventions.

### **Audience Participation**
- Vance invites volunteers to **wave glowing wands** (Minor Illusion wands) and **sing** backup vocals.  
  - **Performance (CHA) DC 12** for a decent showing.  
  - If a PC wants a star cameo, they can do a short comedic duet with Vance (DC 15).  
- If successful, participants get showered with **confetti** and a cameo mention in Vance’s next “GnomeTube” performance.

## 5. **Tomorrow World Invention Displays** (Hosted by Pat Pat)

**Location**: An open-air expo area near **Pat Pat’s Patent Office** train car.

### **Setting & Flavor**

> *Crowds cluster around makeshift stands displaying half-finished gadgets and cunning contraptions. The aroma of burning metal wafts from a corner where a gnome vigorously polishes clockwork wings. Pat Pat, sporting a flamboyant bowler hat and a wide grin, calls out:*

> **“Got an idea? Register it with Pat Pat—where your invention meets its future!”**

### **Pat Pat’s Bulletin Board**
- Showcases the “**Idea of the Week**”: *Calvin Cookie’s Cookie Gun.* Everyone’s abuzz with talk of *“edible ammo”* and *“dessert-based deterrence.”*

### **Displays**  
1. **Hover-Hat Helios**  
   - A metallic top hat with mini-propellers. Demonstrator claims it can levitate the user up to 10 ft for short durations. *But a DC 14 Arcana check reveals frequent engine sputters…*
2. **Thermal Coil Boots**  
   - Self-heating footwear. *DC 12 Investigation check* to note they overheat occasionally, *scorching floors.*  
3. **Squeakless Dynamo**  
   - A “silent” generator that ironically makes more noise than a standard one.  
4. **Cog-to-Go Portable Kitchen**  
   - A foldout stove with multiple gear-driven attachments—chopping, stirring, etc. *Ideal for dwarven feasts on the go.*  

**Optional Skill Checks**  
- **Intelligence (Investigation or Tinker’s Tools) DC 14** to grasp how each invention works.  
- PCs can pitch **their own** invention to Pat Pat for comedic effect or potential future plot hooks.

## 6. **Gnomish Opera**

**Location**: A modestly grand but temporary Opera House car near the Clocktower. Plush seats inside are decorated with swirling gear motifs.

### **Scene Setting**

> *Cascading curtains of velvet shimmer under an array of mechanical chandeliers. Gnome performers in elaborate steampunk costumes take the stage. The score is in “i minor,” an odd tonal key that resonates with arcane undertones.*

### **Performance**  
- The Opera weaves a tale of **forbidden love** between an inventor and a faulty automaton. Dramatic solos include mechanical clanks for percussion.
- **Any PC** who wants to cameo as an opera extra can make a **Performance (CHA) DC 15** to convincingly hit the notes or gracefully navigate the stage’s trapdoors.

> *“Hold me, my clockwork heart, for I spin with longing in the gloom of parted gears…”*  
> The audience dabs away tears at the tragic refrain.

## 7. **Cheer Meister Vote**  

A nod to *How the Grinch Stole Christmas.* The Cheer Meister is chosen for their *“Holiday Spirit and Great Gusto!”*

### **Scene Setting & Mechanics**
- A public vote by secret ballot (some may bribe or trick the process).
- The winner receives:
  1. **The Gaudy Crown of Jubilation** – An obnoxious magic crown that glows bright green.  
     - **Side effect**: Once worn, it sporadically **amplifies the wearer’s voice** at random intervals (DM’s discretion).  
  2. **Coupon Book** – Filled with 101 random coupons for Tinkertown shops. Ranges from *“Buy 1 Get 1 Half Off”* at SoSo Mart to *“Free Cheese Sample at Munster Mart”* to *“One Complimentary Clockwork Carriage Ride”*.
- Skill checks:
  - PCs can attempt **Persuasion (CHA) DC 15** or **Performance (CHA) DC 15** to rally support for a chosen candidate. 
  - Alternatively, **Sleight of Hand (DEX) DC 14** could be used for comedic tampering with ballots—though that might get them in trouble.

> *Mayor Cobblebot officiates, exclaiming, “We crown thee, Cheer Meister of Tinkertown!” as the crown’s glow intensifies to near-blinding.*  
> A wave of giggles and applause follows the new Cheer Meister’s random voice echo.

## 8. **Hanging Gems off the Clocktower & Founding of Tinkertown Story**

**Location**: The Tinkertown Clocktower stands tall at the center, gear-laden spire visible from across the rolling city.

### **Scene Setting**

> *Soft snow drifts downward as an elder gnome steps forward, carrying a crystalline gem. The flickering lamplights cast shards of color across the crowd. Families hush, eyes locked on the Clocktower’s pinnacle. A hush of reverence falls…*

### **Short Founding Story**
> “A century ago,” begins **Ezmirelda Emerald**, her voice echoing across the hush,  
> “Our ancestors, the **EARS**—Experimental Augmentation Research Society—found themselves in Gnormandy, bound by tyrannical rules. Some caved, becoming **GEARS**, slaves to corporate whim.  
> But others believed in *freedom to invent*. So they created the **first hex track**—a repeating runic pattern that let the very ground become our rails.  
> **Tinkertown** was born from their unwavering spirit. We set off, our homes rolling behind us, forging fresh horizons daily. We learned that true invention blossoms only when unshackled—an idea that no government can chain.  
> To honor that birth, we hang these gems upon the Clocktower, each a symbol of *hope, creativity, and unstoppable progress*.”

At that final word, **Ezmirelda** climbs a ladder (or a gear-lift), affixes the gem, and a **warm glow** envelops the tower momentarily.

> *Applause erupts, and a wave of communal pride ripples through Tinkertown.*
