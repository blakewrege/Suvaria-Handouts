Wetwood's Brokeback Outfitters¶

Get High Together!¶

High-country adventure, discreet trail guiding, romantic wilderness logistics, and legally separate underground recreation.
Proprietor: Clint Wetwood, “the Otter with No Shame”
Above Ground, Underground, and Legally in the Clear.

From Western Livery Row to the peaks above Armskirk, Clint Wetwood provides sturdy horses, handsome guides, matching chaps, secluded cabins, and the professional confidence to assure you that every cliff is prettier from the correct side.

Choose Your Adventure¶

Package Includes Price
Get High Together Half-day mountain ride, scenic overlooks, trail lunch, and one completely natural opportunity to hold onto somebody 6 gp per guest
Sunset Ram-Watching Evening ride, blanket, spiced cider or wine, and binoculars powerful enough to identify several kinds of horn 8 gp per guest
Confirmed Bachelor Overnight Private cabin, supper, breakfast, matching chaps, and discretion maintained until the bard learns a rhyme 15 gp per guest
One Bedroll Discount Deducted from any overnight booking when two consenting adults voluntarily reduce the company's laundry expenses −2 gp per pair
Lovers’ Lookout Rescue Ride Carriage or horseback transport, picnic basket, emergency flowers, and one rehearsed apology 5 gp per couple
Discreet Trail Consultation A private route through hills, canals, flood cuts, or old service roads; destination not judged aloud 10 gp per group

Brokeback Family Adventures¶

Excursion Includes Price
Putty Nutty Caverns Guided family spelunking, glittering mineral pockets, echo chambers, natural slides, safety helmet, and the chance to earn an “I Got Putty Nutty!” ribbon 5 gp adult; 2 gp child
The Probably Safe Route Rope descents, tight passages, underground water, advanced waivers, and a guide trained to say “that sound is normal” 12 gp per guest
Private Cavern Booking Closed tour, lantern supper, matching helmets, and nobody asking why you wanted the cave to yourselves 40 gp per group

Important legal truth: Putty Nutty Caverns has absolutely no connection to the thoroughly discredited rumor that a visitor died there. The tour company won the court case, and courts are famously never wrong.

“Bareback is a personal decision, honey. Insurance company still requires saddles.” — Clint Wetwood

Booking¶

Reserve at Wetwood's Brokeback Outfitters, Western Livery Row. After-hours bookings are accepted at the Pink Pony Inn, usually near the bar, the dance floor, or whichever handsome stranger Clint is explaining trail safety to.

Deposits are negotiable. Prices may be reduced for heroic rescues, premium gossip, excellent dancing, or customers Clint finds professionally distracting.

What happens above the tree line stays above the tree line—unless the bard writes a song.
