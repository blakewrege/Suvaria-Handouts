# Excalibur, the Promised Dawn

*Legendary (scaling), requires attunement by a good-aligned creature blessed by an Ancient Dragon*

> **How you got it:** When a Ringwraith descended on Tinkertown, **Saskia the Golden** hurled this blade to **Krunk** as she took wing to battle her corrupted child above. After the fight she vowed to return “on the eve of the last battle”… and, yes, admitted she has the hots for you. Very Arthurian. Very epic.

---

## 📜 Description

A lean, unadorned longsword of pale-gold steel that hums like a held breath before dawn. Its fuller glows with a tiny script—an **Integrity %** that flickers like starlight. 
When drawn, light folds around the edge like a sunrise caught in glass; when sheathed, the light settles into a quiet halo at the throat of the scabbard.
The sword’s voice is bright and earnest, a knightly cheer in your head: *“Stand tall, Sir Krunkulous. I am Excalibur, the Promised Dawn—forged in Avalon by six great fairies as a weapon for the world itself. Draw me when the many must be saved, and together we’ll cut the night and bring the morning.”*

## Quick Facts

* **Type:** Longsword (versatile 1d8/1d10)
* **Light:** Sheds bright light 20 ft. / dim 20 ft. *(unless muted by the scabbard)*
* **Languages while attuned:** Celestial & Draconic
* **Sentience:** Lawful good; INT 8, WIS 14, CHA 16; telepathy with wielder (60 ft.)

* **Personality**: Noble knight, with some quirks. Enthusiastically pro-smite. Favorite title for you: **“Sir Krunkulous the Unclonklable.”**
* **Self-Limiting Radiance:** The blade throttles its own output to avoid killing a mortal bearer; higher unlocks gently loosen the limiters.

## Scaling Enhancement

* **+1** to attack & damage and Spell DCs **Level 5–8**
* **+2** to attack & damage and Spell DCs **Level 9–14** *(you are here)*
* **+3** to attack & damage and Spell DCs **Level 15+**

## Core Features

### Radiant Edge

* Once on each of your turns when you hit with Excalibur, you may deal extra **radiant damage equal to your proficiency bonus**—**but only to creatures that the sword deems evil.**

* Once per turn, when you use any **Smite**, add **+1d8 radiant** damage.

### Oathbound Light

If you commit a vile act (e.g., betray a sacred oath), Excalibur’s abilites are **suppressed for an indefinite period** and it becomes a regular longsword. Expect a lecture about abusing your powers.

### Anti-Theft Protocol

If a non-authorized creature grasps the hilt:

* **“UNAUTHORIZED WIELDER!”** booms in Common/Draconic/Celestial within **300 ft.**
* The sword counts as a **mundane longsword** for them and forces a **DC 25 CON save** or they take **2d6 radiant** and drop it.

### Noble Phantasm — **Promised Victory** *(Anti-Fortress Class)*

**1/long rest.** As an **Action**, expend a **3rd-level spell slot or higher** to fire a daybreak beam: a **15-ft-wide, 120-ft-long** line. Creatures in the line make a **DEX save vs your spell DC**.

* **Fail:** **5d10 radiant + 5d10 force + 2d10 per slot level above 3rd**.
* **Success:** Half.
* **Anti-Fortress:** Non-artifact barriers, heavy fortifications, and magical darkness along the line are seared/suppressed at the DM’s discretion.

**Aftershock — Hero’s Fatigue:** For **1 minute** after firing: **speed halved**, **disadvantage on attack rolls**. A **Lesser Restoration** or **Greater Restoration** can end this early.

### Critical Glory *(Unlocks at Level 15)*

On a critical hit against a **fiend, undead, or shadow creature**, add **+2d8 radiant**. The target must succeed on a **CON save vs your spell DC** or be **blinded** until the end of your next turn.

## Why You Probably Won’t Always Use Excalibur

* **Beacon:** Drawn blade = visible glow. You have disadvantage on Stealth checks and can’t benefit from being Invisible vs. creatures relying on sight.
* **Divine Signature:** Its holy aura can be sensed or scryed from afar by powerful foes.
* **Selective Power:** **Radiant Edge** only bites true evil.
* **Knightly Temperament:** Hates cheap shots and bullying; will *loudly* scold if you swing at weaklings or non-hostiles.
* **Worthy Foe Bias:** Prefers champions and monsters, not “cabbages.” Expect commentary.
* **Attention Magnet:** Open carry makes subtle plans harder; consider **Muted Majesty** or the **Refraction Shroud** when unlocked.

## Avalon: The Everdistant Utopia *(paired wondrous item)*

A rose-linen scabbard banded in summer-gold. **Integrity %** shows in the fuller when drawn (e.g., *“Integrity 67% — Invisible Air: OFFLINE.”*). While worn and **you are not wielding a shield**, you gain **+1 AC**.

### Scabbard’s Mercy *(1/short rest; automatic)*

**If the creature holding the scabbard (including you) is still alive but below or would be reduced to below 0 hit points**, that creature instead becomes **stable at 0 hit points** (unconscious; no death saves).
*You may give the scabbard to an ally—if they’re holding it, the effect can protect them instead.*

### Muted Majesty *(constant, togglable)*

**Bonus Action while sheathed.** Excalibur emits **no light**, reads as **nonmagical** to casual scans, and is hidden from most “detect item” divinations. If drawn while in this mode, then it is a regular longsword until **the start of your next turn** as it needs to power up from standby mode.

### **Fairyward Pavilion** *(Unlocks at **Level 13**; 1/long rest)*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute
**Duration:** **1 hour** (no concentration)
**Range:** 30 ft. (centered on Avalon)

At your call, the world hushes and a **secluded meadow** blossoms into being: a **30-ft-radius sphere** where **warm sunlight** streams from no visible sun and **pink–violet blossoms** drift in slow, weightless arcs. Petals skim the air like starlight fallen from the **inner sea**, and the distant outline of a **lone tower** seems to watch over the field’s edge. The scabbard must remain **within 30 ft.** of the center; if it leaves, the field ends.

**Bounded Field effects:**

* **Muffling Veil.** Sound across the boundary is dulled; conversations inside can’t be clearly heard outside.
* **One-Way Haze.** From outside, the sphere appears as a **sun-washed bower** carpeted in flowers and gently drifting petals, **heavily obscuring** what lies within. From inside, you see out normally, with occasional visions of **petal-showers and tiny fairy folk** dancing at the periphery.
* **Divination Dampers.** **Scrying sensors** can’t appear inside, and creatures within can’t be **targeted by divination**.
* **No Blinks, No Planes.** **Teleportation and planar travel** can’t occur **into or out of** the field.
* **Gentle Repulse.** Hostile creatures with **CR lower than your character level** can’t willingly enter by nonmagical means. Hostile creatures of CR **equal to or higher** than your level must first succeed on a **Charisma save** against **your spell save DC** to enter. The field is **difficult terrain** for hostile creatures within it.

**Healing & solace within:**

* **Summer’s Mend.** At the **end of each of your turns**, All living creatures in the field each regain **1d6** hit points.

* **First Sip of Dawn.** The **first time a living creature enters the field during this casting**, it **recovers one 1st-level spell slot *or* removes one level of exhaustion**. 

* **Sweet Balm of Avalon.** All living creatures within the field have **advantage on saves vs both magical and nonmagical ailments**. Ongoing **disease** is **suppressed** while a creature remains in the field.

> **Lore — Echo of the Garden:**
> Once, Avalon sealed its chosen in a perfect sanctuary—**a garden the world could not touch**. That absolute defense has faded, but this gentler **Pavilion** lingers: **sunlit petals, quiet air, and blessings that mend**, like a memory of paradise glimpsed from the **four corners** of a weary battlefield.
