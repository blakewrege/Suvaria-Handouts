# Dragon Child Toy Shop

## Toys, Curios, Climbers, and Constructive Ways to Exhaust a Child

**Longzi Wanwu Pu** carries toys for humanoid children, beastfolk children, animal companions, household pets, and adults purchasing very elaborate gifts “for a niece.” Custom colors, family crests, reinforced construction, and species-appropriate sizing are available.

> **A polite shop notice:** Catfolk are people, housecats are cats, and neither appreciates being handed catnip without being asked. Madam Lian Zhu has survived this conversation before.

## Magical Toy Notes

The magical toys are harmless curiosities, common-quality trinkets, or minor utilities. None are combat-ready or powerful enough to solve a serious problem unless the problem is boredom, mild darkness, or a child crying in a restaurant.

Prices are negotiable for repairs, trade-ins, good stories, and customers who rescue loose dragon marbles without stepping on anyone.
