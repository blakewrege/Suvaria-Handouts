### Bag of Seed Tricks (Plants vs Zombies Edition)

<div class="portal-note"><strong>Archive audit notice:</strong> This handout was released before Session 077 and may contain older mechanics, names, links, or lore phrasing that still needs review. The table's latest ruling is authoritative.</div>


This enchanted bag appears to be filled with ordinary seeds, but when planted, they grow into various plants from the "Plants vs Zombies" game. The bag weighs 1/2 pound.

- **Usage:** You can use an action to pull 1-3 seeds from the bag and throw them up to 20 feet . Roll a d8 to determine which plant grows, using the table below. The vanishes at the next dawn or when reduced to 0 hit points.
- **Limit:** Three seeds can be planted per day, resetting at dawn.

**d8 Table:**

1. **Peashooter:** Fires peas at enemies within range.
2. **Sunflower:** Generates extra action points (or equivalent resource) for one turn.
3. **Melon-pult:** Does AoE damage
4. **Wall-nut:** Creates a sturdy barrier.
5. **Spikeweed:** Burrows and attacks when an enemy steps on it.
6. **Ice-shroom :** Shoots freezing spores, slowing enemies.
7. **Chomper:** Can bite and swallow a nearby enemy.
8. **Squash:** Jumps and squashes an enemy for heavy damage.

### Peashooter - CR 0.5  

*Small Plant*

- **AC:** 13 (natural armor)
- **HP:** 18 (4d8)
- **Speed:** 0 ft.
- **STR:** 6 (-2), **DEX:** 14 (+2), **CON:** 10 (+0), **INT:** 2 (-4), **WIS:** 12 (+1), **CHA:** 6 (-2)
- **Vulnerabilities:** Fire
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 11
- **Abilities:**
  - **Photosynthesis:** Regains 1 HP at the start of its turn if in sunlight.
  - **Rooted:** The Peashooter cannot be moved or knocked prone.
- **Actions:**
  - **Pea Shot:** Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, range 30/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) piercing damage.


---

### Sunflower - CR 0.5  
*Small Plant*


- **AC:** 12
- **HP:** 16 (3d8 + 3)
- **Speed:** 0 ft.
- **STR:** 3 (-4), **DEX:** 8 (-1), **CON:** 12 (+1), **INT:** 2 (-4), **WIS:** 15 (+2), **CHA:** 10 (+0)
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 12
- **Abilities:**
  - **Sunlight Blessing:** As a bonus action, the Sunflower can grant 5 temporary hit points to one creature within 30 feet that it can see.
  - **Healing Light:** Twice per day, the Sunflower can cast *Cure Wounds* at level 1 with a range of 30 ft.
  - **Solor Powered:** Once per day, the Sunflower can release all of its stored energy to allow a caster to regain one spell slot at any level during a short rest.
- **Rooted:** The Sunflower cannot be moved or knocked prone.

### Wall-nut - CR 0.5
*Large Plant, takes 2 squares*

- **AC:** 17 (natural armor)
- **HP:** 38 (7d8 + 14)
- **Speed:** 0 ft.
- **STR:** 14 (+2), **DEX:** 5 (-3), **CON:** 15 (+2), **INT:** 2 (-4), **WIS:** 8 (-1), **CHA:** 3 (-4)
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Slashing
- **Condition Immunities:** Blinded, Deafened, Prone
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 9
- **Abilities:**
  - **Sturdy Defense:** The Wall-nut provides three-quarters cover to allies behind it.
  - **Rooted:** The Wall-nut cannot be moved or knocked prone.
  - **Regrowth:** If not destroyed, the Wall-nut regains 5 hit points at the start of its turn.

**Actions:**

- **(None)**

The Wall-nut is designed to be a defensive creature, providing cover and absorbing damage, but unable to make attacks.

### Melon-pult - CR 1
*Medium Plant*

- **AC:** 13
- **HP:** 22 (5d8)
- **Speed:** 0 ft.
- **STR:** 5 (-3), **DEX:** 12 (+1), **CON:** 13 (+1), **INT:** 2 (-4), **WIS:** 10 (+0), **CHA:** 6 (-2)
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire
- **Condition Immunities:** Blinded, Deafened, Prone
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 10
- **Abilities:**
  - **Splash Damage:** When the Melon-pult hits with its melon attack, all creatures within 5 feet of the target must make a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking half the melon attack's damage on a failed save.
- **Rooted:** The Melon-pult cannot be moved or knocked prone.

**Actions:**

- **Melon Attack:** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, range 60/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (4d6+1) bludgeoning damage.

### Spikeweed - CR 1
*Large Plant, can be walked over*

- **AC:** 13
- **HP:** 26 (4d8 + 8)
- **Speed:** 0 ft.
- **STR:** 8 (-1), **DEX:** 14 (+2), **CON:** 15 (+2), **INT:** 2 (-4), **WIS:** 10 (+0), **CHA:** 3 (-4)
- **Damage Resistances:** Piercing, Slashing
- **Condition Immunities:** Blinded, Deafened, Prone
- **Senses:** Tremorsense 10 ft., Passive Perception 10
- **Abilities:**
  - **Ground Trap:** Spikeweed can hide underground, becoming invisible to creatures without tremorsense.
  - **Difficult Terrain:** When not hidden, the space Spikeweed occupies becomes difficult terrian.
  - **Rooted:** The Spikeweed cannot be moved or knocked prone.
- **Actions:**
  - **Spiked Grasp:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature that steps on it. *Hit:* 12 (3d6+2) piercing damage.

Spikeweed is designed to be an ambush predator, hiding and attacking when an enemy steps on it. Its attack is potent against those who fail to detect it.

### Ice-shroom - CR 1
*Medium Plant*


- **AC:** 12
- **HP:** 24 (4d8 + 8)
- **Speed:** 0 ft.
- **STR:** 3 (-4), **DEX:** 10 (+0), **CON:** 14 (+2), **INT:** 2 (-4), **WIS:** 11 (+0), **CHA:** 3 (-4)
- **Damage Resistances:** Cold
- **Condition Immunities:** Blinded, Deafened, Prone
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 10
- **Abilities:**
  - **Chill Aura:** The Ice-shroom emits a chilling aura in a 10-foot radius. Any enemy that starts its turn in this area has its speed halved for that turn.
  - **Rooted:** The Ice-shroom cannot be moved or knocked prone.
- **Actions:**
  - **Frost Breath:** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +5 to hit, range 30 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (2d6) cold damage. The target's speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of the Ice-shroom's next turn.

The Ice-shroom is designed to control the battlefield, slowing enemies and dealing cold damage, making it effective against groups of foes.

### Chomper - CR 2
*Large Plant*

- **AC:** 13
- **HP:** 36 (8d6 + 8)
- **Speed:** 0 ft.
- **STR:** 18 (+4), **DEX:** 8 (-1), **CON:** 14 (+2), **INT:** 2 (-4), **WIS:** 10 (+0), **CHA:** 4 (-3)
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire
- **Condition Immunities:** Blinded, Deafened, Prone
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 10
- **Abilities:**
  - **Swallow Whole:** On a critical hit with its bite attack, or if it successfully grapples a target, the Chomper can swallow the target whole. The swallowed creature is blinded and restrained, and it takes 10 (3d6) acid damage at the start of each of the Chomper's turns. The Chomper can have only one creature swallowed at a time.
  - **Rooted:** The Chomper cannot be moved or knocked prone.
- **Actions:**
  - **Bite:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) piercing damage. As part of this attack you may attempt to grapple a creature medium or smaller.

- **Speed:** 10 ft.
- **STR:** 18 (+4), **DEX:** 6 (-2), **CON:** 16 (+3), **INT:** 2 (-4), **WIS:** 8 (-1), **CHA:** 3 (-4)
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire
- **Condition Immunities:** Blinded, Deafened, Prone
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 9
- **Abilities:**
- **Actions:**
  - **Crush:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 22 (4d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage. On hit a medium or smaller target is knocked prone and restrained as long as Squash is on top of them or until they succeed a escape check.
