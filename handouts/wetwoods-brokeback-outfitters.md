# Wetwood's Brokeback Outfitters
## Get High Together!

> **High-country adventure, discreet trail guiding, romantic wilderness logistics, and legally separate underground recreation.**<br>
> Proprietor: **Clint Wetwood, “the Otter with No Shame”**<br>
> **Above Ground, Underground, and Legally in the Clear.**

From Western Livery Row to the peaks above Armskirk, Clint Wetwood provides sturdy horses, handsome guides, matching chaps, secluded cabins, and the professional confidence to assure you that every cliff is prettier from the correct side.

## Booking

Reserve at **Wetwood's Brokeback Outfitters, Western Livery Row**. After-hours bookings are accepted at the **Pink Pony Inn**, usually near the bar, the dance floor, or whichever handsome stranger Clint is explaining trail safety to.

Deposits are negotiable. Prices may be reduced for heroic rescues, premium gossip, excellent dancing, or customers Clint finds professionally distracting.
