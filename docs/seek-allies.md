**Greetings, Adventurers!**

Tinkertown breathes again, but the world beyond its steam-belching rails is far from still.  
The **Eternal Engine** is turning—but it won’t turn the tide alone.

The **Council of Chaos**—an ancient pact of Suvaria’s darkest powers—has begun to stir.  
Whether they see you as a threat… or a pawn… remains unclear.  
But the ground beneath your boots is shifting.

**It’s time to Seek Allies.**

Near the start of the next session, you’ll choose your destination.  
Each path opens new possibilities—and carries its own risks.

---

### 🧁 1. King Curry’s Hidden Truths

**Aunt Jill Cookie**, and the women and children of King Curry’s warren, were taken to a forced labor camp in the **Yundabad Mountains**, near the orc city of **Cheddud Bha**.  
Curry knows where—but not what’s being built.

> *“It hums like thunder and eats the sky.”*

To reach it, you’ll cross lands where **Sauron’s name is law**, and shadows walk in daylight.

- Crude maps and escape routes scratched on old parchment  
- Rumors of a fortress so large it blots out the sun  
- A single warning: *“Not even the shadows come back from that place.”*

---

### 🧙‍♀️ 2. Invitation to Hügsnorts

A **bat-winged cat wearing a monocle** delivers a glittering letter:

> *“You are hereby invited to tea, tutelage, and perhaps a touch of destiny.”*

The **School of Hexcraft & Hubbub** has summoned **Amaryllis**.  
The drifting city of **Brithombar** hides behind layers of illusion—neutral in every war, or so they claim.  
But something hums beneath the stillness… like magic waiting to snap.

- The invitation won’t stop twitching  
- A note on the back: *“Don’t trust the headmaster’s reflection.”*  
- A chance to learn what others are preparing for—or hiding from

---

### 🔥 3. Fires in Ming Lung

**Fang Ro’s homeland burns again.**

A new order—the **Jǐn Bào**, or *“Golden Leopards”*—claims to bring balance through flame.  
Their leader, **Master Zhaoyan**, preaches purity and obedience to the fire.  
Villages vanish. Temples fall silent. The **Mógu Chóng Bài** have gone into hiding.

But Fang has heard this rhetoric before—beneath screams and smoke.  
She ended it once… or thought she did.

- A scroll sealed in red wax bears Fang’s name in Infernal  
- Refugees speak of feline shadows prowling rooftops at dawn  
- The rivers of Mian Lung now run black with ash

---

### 🏯 4. The Trials of the Three Cities

Every five years, the Republic of **Leonia** in the far east holds a ritual competition to choose the next **Ringleader**.    
That time has come.

But something feels different this year.  
Relics stir beneath the arenas. Prophecies resurface.  
And some whisper the **Council’s influence** has already reached the Free Cities.

- The competition is being held in Armskirk this time. An old ally, **Kardarsha the Kajiit**, can get you inside the trials  
- One challenge is called *“The Walk Between Flames”*—no one agrees what it means  
- The winner shapes the cities’ future. The losers may not leave at all
