### Ring of Charm

*Wondrous item, uncommon*

3 charges per day. You may cast [Charm Person](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Charm%20Person#content) (DC 15), but you must shake the target’s hand with your ring hand. The target does not learn you tried to charm it on the first use, regardless of success or failure.

### Talking Skull

*Wondrous item, uncommon*

This skull has forgotten its name and seems to be constantly eyeing you. Once per day you can animate the skull, lighting up its eyes with a pale blue light. Roll a d6 and a d100. While animated, it speaks with you and will answer a question to the best of its ability if you correctly guess a riddle.

### Stasis Module Charm

The current mechanics live in the canonical [Bells — Stasis Module Charm](stasis-module-charm.md) handout.

### Elixir of Enhanced Eloquence

*Wondrous potion, rare (requires attunement)*

A potion that, when consumed, gives the drinker a silver tongue. It gives a permanent +1 to Charisma skill checks.

It also allows the user to cast *Comprehend Languages* once per day without using a spell slot.

> **Legacy text note:** The original sentence ends after “not impacted…” without naming what does not affect the bonus. Use the benefits above as written unless the table establishes the missing limitation.

## Items Recorded Under Caracas

[Open Caracas’s current player record](caracas-record.md).

### Maul of Maelstroms

*Maul, uncommon (requires attunement)*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The bonus damage is lightning damage. Once per long rest you can shout a battle cry and slam an enemy, dealing an extra 1d10 thunder damage. They must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked back 10 feet and prone.

You feel a slight shock whenever you pick it up; the weapon seems to vibrate with energy.

### Garland of Garlic

*Net, uncommon*

A magical garland of garlic laced with silver barbs. On a hit, it wraps around the vampire’s neck and prevents it from using its bite action. It cannot be removed or destroyed by a vampire regardless of shapechanging. It has the same properties as a [Net](https://www.dndbeyond.com/equipment/net), except it does not restrain.

### Bone Breaker

*Mace, uncommon (requires attunement)*

A large spiked mace that says “Bone Breaker, do not use!” hastily written across it.

**Base weapon:** 1d6 bludgeoning, +1 piercing, +1 to hit, magical.

- **Break Bones (Recharge 5–6):** Melee attack. On a hit, the target must succeed on a Constitution save (DC 14) or have its AC reduced by 1 and speed reduced by 10 feet until healed.
- **Hunger for Blood:** At the start of combat the wielder must make a Wisdom save (DC 14). On a failure, you must Dash and Reckless Attack (Barbarian) an enemy within melee range of Bone Breaker.
- **Bloodied Rage:** While the wielder is bloodied in combat this weapon deals an extra 1d4 bludgeoning damage. At the start of their turn the wielder must make a Constitution save (DC 14) to avoid attacking the nearest creature (random if more than one is adjacent).

## Items Recorded Under Krunk

[Open Krunk’s current player record](krunk-record.md).

### Ring of the Gladiators

*Wondrous item, common*

Explosive applause plays every time you critically hit with an attack or deliver a killing blow to an enemy. Add 1d6 to your next attack roll.

A laugh track plays if the wielder critically fails an attack roll. Subtract 1d6 from your next attack roll.

### Veil of Shadows

*Wondrous item, rare*

A large black amulet also known as the Shield of the Void. Once per day you can use a bonus action or reaction to speak this amulet’s command word, creating an aura that lightly obscures and gives resistance to all damage types until your next turn to everything in a 10-foot radius around you.

### Sword of Screams

*Longsword, uncommon*

When pulled from its scabbard, everyone within 60 feet is deafened for 1d4 turns. It has the same statistics as a longsword and is magical. While wielding this weapon you may ask the DM to play selected screamo or metal music.

### Chilling Halberd

*Halberd, uncommon (requires attunement)*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon and can apply the Call of the Grave effect when you hit.

- **Call of the Grave (Recharge 6):** Add 1d6 necrotic damage. A ghostly skeletal hand attaches to the target, and it can’t regain hit points until the start of your next turn. Until then, the hand clings to the target. If you hit an undead target, it also has disadvantage on its next attack roll against you until the start of your next turn.

### Ash’s Righteous Chainsaw Blade

The current mechanics live in the canonical [Krunk — Ash’s Righteous Chainsaw Blade](ashs-righteous-chainsaw-blade.md) handout.

### Historian’s Horologe

*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

This magical pocket watch allows its owner to transport back to a previously set position once per day.

## Former Character Records

These names came from the imported document, but they do not currently have public player ledgers.

### Eyes of the Eagle

> **Historical owner:** Ella

*Wondrous item, uncommon*

While wearing them, you have advantage on Perception and Investigation checks that rely on sight. In conditions of clear visibility, you can make out details of even extremely distant creatures and objects as small as 2 feet across.

### Helm of Invisibility

> **Historical owner:** Ella

*Wondrous item, uncommon*

This helmet makes the wearer’s head invisible. You may use this helm once per day to give yourself advantage on an Intimidation or Deception check.

### Kinesis Module

> **Historical owner:** Ella

*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*

- **Kinesis Grab:** As an action, you can cast *Mage Hand*. The spectral hand created by this spell is invisible.
- **Kinesis Launch (Recharge after a Short Rest):** Once per short rest, you can cast *Catapult* at 4th level without using a spell slot (6d8). The damage dealt by this ability can be modified by the item you choose to throw.

The Kinesis Module, inspired by *Dead Space*, grants the wielder telekinetic abilities. This magical item is perfect for tactically minded characters who wish to manipulate their environment and use objects as weapons in creative ways.

### Boots of Elvenkind

> **Historical owner:** Vex

*Wondrous item, uncommon*

While you wear these boots, your steps make no sound, regardless of the surface you are moving across. You also have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks that rely on moving silently.

### Necklace of Lightning Bolts

> **Historical owner:** Strawberry

*Wondrous guisarme, uncommon — legacy item type preserved as imported*

Once per turn, you can use an action to detach a bead. When you do, a 3rd-level *Lightning Bolt* spell (save DC 14) is created in the direction of your choice.

You can detach multiple beads, or even the whole necklace, as one action. When you do so, increase the level of *Lightning Bolt* by 1 for each bead beyond the first. You may use this while wild shaped if the creature has enough manual dexterity.

## Sold or Unassigned Items

### Shadow Dagger

> **Status:** Sold

*Dagger, rare (requires attunement)*

You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

- **Returning:** Shadow Dagger returns to the wielder’s hand immediately after it is used to make a ranged attack.
- **Backstabber:** Roll an additional 1d4 damage when the target is flat-footed.
- **Create Undead Minion (Long Rest):** Once per long rest you may spend an action to stab the Shadow Dagger into a fresh corpse. The corpse becomes an undead minion and obeys your commands until it is destroyed or you use this ability again. High-CR creatures will be reduced to one.

### Demon Mask

> **Status:** Sold

*Wondrous item, uncommon*

This terrifying mask is crafted in the visage of a leering demon and grants a +1 item bonus to Intimidation checks.

- **Leering Gaze (Long Rest):** The mask casts the *Fear* spell with a DC of 14 (or spell bonus) on a single target that can see it. This doesn’t require concentration from the wearer.

### Shield of Friendship

> **Recorded owner:** Unknown

*Wondrous shield, uncommon*

As a bonus action, you may send your shield to one other creature up to 30 feet away, giving them +2 AC until the start of your next turn. Until the shield returns you do not benefit from its bonus. If your hands are full at the start of your turn, the shield appears at your feet or in a holster you are wearing.

### Horschopper

> **Recorded owner:** Unknown

*Wondrous guisarme, uncommon*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. This magical makeshift +1 guisarme can be wielded with one hand, but deals 1d6 instead of 1d10 when attacking someone 10 feet away.
