# **Order of Events**

1️⃣ **Three-Legged Ice Skating Race** 🏃‍♂️ (*Speed & Agility*) → Leads directly into  
2️⃣ **Mechanical Bobsled Race** 🛷 (*Engineering & Control*) → Finishes at  
3️⃣ **Snow Slingshot Siege** ❄️ (*Strategy & Accuracy!*)

## **Why These Work as a Triathlon**
- **Momentum Carries Over**: The **faster** you finish **one event, the sooner you begin the next**.  
- **Team Roles Matter**: Different skills shine—**athletics for skating, dexterity for bobsledding, intelligence for siege-building**.  
- **Each event has its own chaos** but connects seamlessly—leading to last-second turnarounds!  

## **🏆 Spectator Experience & Commentary Tracks**  
The **Great Tinkertown Winter Triathlon** isn’t just for competitors—it’s **one of the biggest spectator events of the year!**  
A pair of **gnomish sports commentators**—legendary announcers **Riff Gearwhistle** and **Clank Cogsworth**—provide **play-by-play analysis, comedic banter, and exaggerated reactions** to every twist and turn of the race.  

# **Event 1: The Three-Legged Ice Skating Race**  
⛸️ **Team Coordination Meets Sheer Chaos!**  

The **first event of The Great Tinkertown Winter Triathlon** is a classic gnomish spin on ice racing—*literally*. Competitors must **tie one leg to their partner’s**, strap on **skates that may or may not have been recently tested**, and make their way across an **icy obstacle-laden course** without wiping out!  

### **Rules & Structure**
- Each **team of six** must **pair up into three sets of two** for the race.  
- Pairs will **tie one leg together** and attempt to **skate as fast as possible** across the course.  
- Once they **cross the finish line**, they **untie themselves** and **jump straight into their pre-built bobsled** for **Event 2**.  
- **The faster they finish, the sooner they start the bobsled race!**

### **The Ice Course Layout**
The **race spans 300 feet** of **icy terrain**, broken into **four treacherous sections:**  

| **Section**          | **Description** | **Mechanics & DCs** |
|---------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------|
| **1. The Slippery Start (0-50 ft.)** | The first stretch is **pristine but deceptively slick ice.** Racers must find their rhythm fast or risk slipping immediately! | **Acrobatics (DC 10):** Success = Normal movement. Fail = Skid in place, lose 5 feet. |
| **2. The Snowy Patches (50-150 ft.)** | Patches of **light snow mounds** make the surface uneven, forcing teams to **weave carefully or risk getting tripped up.** | **Dexterity (DC 12):** Success = Navigate cleanly. Fail = Stumble, lose 10 feet. |
| **3. The Wind Tunnel (150-250 ft.)** | Strong **gusts from enchanted snow fans** blast racers unpredictably, sending them veering sideways or spinning out. | **Strength (DC 12):** Success = Push through. Fail = Get pushed back 5-10 feet. |
| **4. The Mogul Gauntlet (250-300 ft.)** | A final **series of small icy ramps and dips** threaten to **launch** the unprepared. | **Acrobatics (DC 14):** Success = Land smoothly. Fail = Faceplant at the finish line (but still move forward). |

💡 **DM Option:** Allow **teamwork mechanics**—one partner can use their roll to **help** the other if they’re proficient in Acrobatics!

---

### **Bonus Hazards & Spectator Chaos**
- **The Gnomish Pep Band** 🎺: Over-enthusiastic musicians line the track, occasionally blasting out a discordant tune that might cause distraction (*DC 10 Constitution save or momentary loss of focus*).  
- **Wild Penguins (or Mechanical Equivalents?)** 🐧: Someone let loose a flock of **confused snowy birds** that periodically **slide across the track, forcing emergency dodges** (*DC 13 Dexterity save or collision*).  
- **Loose Magic Snowballs** ❄️: *Random “stray” enchanted snowballs* occasionally bounce onto the track, leaving patches of **slippery or sticky ice**.  

🏁 **Finishing the Race:**  
- The **first teams to cross** gain **priority access to their bobsleds** in **Event 2**.  
- **Slower teams** must scramble into their sleds **seconds behind**, putting them at a small disadvantage!

# **Event 2: The Mechanical Bobsled Race**  
🛷 **Speed, Stability, and Tinkertown Engineering!**  

The second event **tests raw speed, teamwork, and craftsmanship** as teams **pilot their pre-built bobsleds** down an **icy, winding course**. However, these sleds have **no built-in propulsion**—meaning **gravity, weight distribution, and steering skill** determine victory!

---

### **Bobsled Construction Rules**
- Each **team of six** had time **before the race** to **design and build their sled** using **non-magical materials**.  
- Sleds **must be piloted by four teammates at a time** (*two will rotate out and join for the next event*).  
- Teams were **allowed to build for either speed, stability, or gimmicks**, affecting how well they perform on the track!  

💡 **Strategy Choices:**  
| **Build Focus**  | **Effect** |
|----------------|-----------------------------|
| **Speed-Focused Build** 🏎️ | Bonus to initial momentum, but **steering is harder**. |
| **Stability-Focused Build** 🏗️ | Handles turns well, but might **lag behind** in acceleration. |
| **Weird Gnome Engineering™ Build** 🤪 | Could be brilliant… or explode. DM rolls secretly. |

### **The Icy Track Layout**
The **bobsled run** is a **500-foot course** featuring **sharp turns, unexpected jumps, and environmental hazards**. **Successful teams** must **balance speed and control** while **navigating obstacles.**

| **Section**           | **Description** | **Mechanics & DCs** |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------|---------------------|
| **1. The Drop (0-100 ft.)** | The first **50-foot near-vertical slope** launches sleds into high speeds FAST. | **Athletics (DC 12) to brace or lose control.** |
| **2. The Frostbite Chicane (100-250 ft.)** | A sharp set of twists **force teams to steer carefully** or risk a wipeout. | **Dexterity (DC 14) to steer safely.** |
| **3. The Mogul Madness (250-350 ft.)** | **Ice humps and gaps** shake sleds, testing suspension. Poor builds might crack here! | **Tinker’s Tools (DC 13) for durability checks.** |
| **4. The Avalanche Curve (350-450 ft.)** | A **steep banking turn** requires perfect balance—or you flip! | **Acrobatics (DC 15) to keep upright.** |
| **5. The Final Stretch (450-500 ft.)** | The fastest section—**raw speed vs. last-second steering!** | **Final Dexterity (DC 12) OR Strength (DC 14) for a last-second push!** |

---

### **Bonus Hazards & Mayhem**
- **Gnomish "Air Fans" Malfunction** 🍃: Automated fans *meant* to reduce wind resistance occasionally **fire randomly**, boosting or spinning sleds out (*DC 13 Dexterity save*).  
- **Tumbling Snow Golems** ⛄: A **runaway sentient snowball** *somehow* found its way onto the track. A team must **dodge it or get slowed down** (*DC 14 Acrobatics check*).  
- **Sabotage or Defensive Tactics?** 🤔: Teams can **lean into their build strategy** to mess with rivals—use extra weight to **bump opponents off course** (*contested Strength check*) or **fake a steering malfunction** (*Deception vs. Insight*).  

🏁 **Finishing the Race:**  
- **First to finish = First to start building their siege launcher in Event 3!**  
- **Slower teams get a delayed start**, meaning less time to build and launch!

# **Event 3: The Snow Slingshot Siege**  
🎯 **Precision, Power, and a Dash of Chaos!**  

The **final event** of the **Great Tinkertown Winter Triathlon** tests not just speed, but ingenuity! After hurtling through the **Three-Legged Ice Skating Race** and **Mechanical Bobsled Race**, teams **immediately** enter a fortified arena where they must **construct and use a massive snow-launching contraption** to knock down targets before their opponents do!  

Teams must **build their siege weapon from provided materials**, then **fire at six magical targets** to claim victory. **Faster teams from the previous event get a head start on construction.**  

🏁 **First team to destroy all their targets wins the Triathlon!**  

---

## **🏗️ Phase 1: Construction (Time-Based Head Start)**
- **Each team must construct** a snow-launching device:  
  - **Slingshot** (Quick-firing, but lower power).  
  - **Catapult** (Good range, moderate accuracy).  
  - **Trebuchet** (Most powerful, but slowest to reload).  
- **Faster bobsled teams get a head start** based on their **finish order**:  
  - 🥇 **1st place in bobsled race** = **10-minute head start**  
  - 🥈 **2nd place** = **5-minute head start**  
  - 🥉 **3rd place or later** = **No delay, start immediately**  

### **🔧 Building Mechanics**
- Teams **describe their design** and assign roles.  
- **Each player contributes one skill check** (or helps another).  
- **Three key elements determine launcher quality**:  

| **Skill Check** | **Effect** |
|----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Intelligence (Tinker’s Tools, Arcana, or Investigation) DC 13+** | **Design stability** – Reduces chance of misfires. |
| **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand or Tinker’s Tools) DC 14+** | **Fine-tuning aim** – Improves accuracy on target shots. |
| **Strength (Athletics or Raw Construction) DC 12+** | **Power & durability** – Prevents breaking under stress. |

🎲 *DM Note:* If the team rolls exceptionally well (all rolls DC 18+), their launcher gains a **+1 bonus** on all attack rolls. If they fail multiple checks (more than 2 failures), it starts **wobbly** and has a **10% chance of misfiring** on every shot.

## **🎯 Phase 2: Target Practice (Firing the Siege Weapon!)**
Once built, teams must **fire at 6 magical targets** to win.  

Each turn:  
1. A player **loads the launcher** (no check required).  
2. A player **aims & fires** (Attack Roll required).  
3. The shot **hits or misses** based on target AC.  

| **Target Type** | **AC** | **Special Rule** |
|---------------|------|------------------------------------------------|
| **Wooden Dummy** (Basic Target) | 12 | Simple, stationary, easy to hit. |
| **Enchanted Floating Gear** | 14 | Floats erratically, **requires Dex Save (DC 12) on hit to confirm direct impact**. |
| **Defensive Ice Wall** | 16 | **Requires a Strength-based attack** to break through. |
| **Animated Snowman** | 14 | **Moves 5 feet after every hit**, forcing new angles. |
| **Bouncing Bell Target** | 18 | **Disadvantage on attack rolls unless using a powerful launch method**. |
| **Final “Grand Clocktower” Target** | 20 | **Must be hit twice** to fully destroy! |

💡 **Tactical Options:**  
- **Fast-firing Slingshot**: More chances to hit, but may struggle with armored/moving targets.  
- **Steady Catapult**: Balanced power and accuracy.  
- **Heavy Trebuchet**: Devastating force, but **takes 2 rounds to reload**.  

🔧 **Adjustments & Fixes:**  
- A team member can **spend a round** fixing issues if their launcher misfires (DC 12 Intelligence or Tinker’s Tools).  
- Teams can **switch shooters** if one is struggling.  

🚨 **Winning Condition:**  
**First team to destroy all 6 targets wins the Triathlon!**  

---

## **🎭 Bonus Chaos & Special Mechanics**
To keep the event **dynamic and unpredictable**, the following **random events** may occur:  

| **Random Event (Roll d6)** | **Effect** |
|---------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| **1 - Slippery Ice Patch** | Someone slips near the launcher! Next shooter rolls **Dex Save DC 13** or **has Disadvantage**. |
| **2 - Referee Interference** | A gnomish referee runs onto the field screaming **“Foul!”** before realizing their mistake. **Delays one shot.** |
| **3 - Sudden Blizzard** | Visibility drops! All **attack rolls made at Disadvantage** until the storm passes (1d3 rounds). |
| **4 - Mechanical Malfunction** | A random team’s launcher **misfires**, shooting backward instead of forward (must be reset). |
| **5 - Rival Sabotage!** | Another team’s gnome assistant sneaks up and **loosens a hinge**—if unnoticed, next shot is **wildly inaccurate**. (Perception DC 14 to catch them!) |
| **6 - Surprise Extra Target!** | A new **floating “bonus target” (AC 15)** appears! Hitting it grants a **+1 to all future attack rolls.** |

---

## **🏆 Why This Works as the Final Event**
- **Speed matters, but so does skill** – Rushing through earlier events gets you a head start, but a **poorly built launcher could ruin your chances.**  
- **Different team members shine in different areas** – Intelligence for design, Dexterity for aiming, Strength for power.  
- **Spectator-friendly excitement** – **Misfires, random magic mishaps, and comeback moments keep the crowd entertained!**  
- **A tense, neck-and-neck ending** – Since teams fire at different speeds and accuracy varies, **no one is ever truly out of the race until the final shot!**  

---

### **Final Order of Events in The Great Tinkertown Winter Triathlon**
1️⃣ **Three-Legged Ice Skating Race** ⛸️ (*Agility & Coordination*) → Teams rush to their bobsleds.  
2️⃣ **Mechanical Bobsled Race** 🛷 (*Speed & Engineering*) → Finish order determines Siege building time.  
3️⃣ **The Snow Slingshot Siege** ❄️ (*Strategy, Teamwork, & Accuracy*) → **First team to destroy their targets wins!**
