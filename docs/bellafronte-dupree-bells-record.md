## Known Background

Bellafronte Dupree—**Bells** to those granted the privilege—is a human noblewoman from **White Rock**. She was raised on the Dupree family estate beside the White Mountains, where the **Dupree Diamond Company** owns mines, quarries, and enough local influence to make municipal government feel like an optional accessory.

Bells dresses in her best finery regardless of weather, danger, or common sense. Large hats, elaborate dresses, gold, diamonds, and pearls are everyday wear. Her fanciful southern accent is unmistakable; whether it is entirely genuine remains one of White Rock’s less profitable mysteries.

She was educated by prestigious and extremely expensive tutors. Her magical training ended when a former court wizard discovered that no salary was sufficient compensation for teaching her. Bells continued practicing on her own, which is brave of everyone nearby.

Her family includes her father **Edmund Dupree**, her stepmother **Collette Dupree**, her younger brother **Leopold**, and her remarkably dim cousin **Dimwood**. She also owns a small vineyard in **Eowadele**.
