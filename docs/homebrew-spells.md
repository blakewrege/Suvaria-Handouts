# Reward Spells
**Note:** Reward spells require no spell slot unless specified.

![Chromatic magical sparks](https://i.imgur.com/XxFA6Oz.png)

## Crowlies Cromatic Sparks
*transmutation spell*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, M, S (bit of glitter)
- **Duration:** Up to 3 hours
- **Uses:** 1 per short rest

This spell is similar to [skywrite](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/skywrite) except it works a lot better at night since it's just fireworks without the noise. You can also write on buildings and clothing. The smaller the writing the longer it lasts. The color of the sparks is affected by the color of the glitter used.
**Level 5**: Writing on a surface now lasts 24 hours and you can weave intricate shapes into your writing.

## Wild Shrooms
*conjuration spell*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, M, S (dirt)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Uses:** 1 per long rest

As an action, while in the forest or underground you touch the dirt and call upon the Mycelium spirits to come to your aid! Suddenly a ring of mushrooms up to 20ft in diameter appears in the designated location.  Anyone that walks within a foot of the ring is affected by the mushroom's spores. The size and effects of the mushrooms are determined by what kind they are. (best start studying those now...)
**Level 5**: Assuming you have been casting this, you are now proficient in mushroom knowledge and  have a better idea of what kind of mushrooms you are calling forth.

## Animated Shroom
*conjuration spell*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 bonus action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, M, S (dirt, mushroom spore)
- **Duration:** Up to 1 hour
- **Uses:** 1 per long rest

As an action, you instantly grow and animate a mushroom that you control. The mushroom takes on the appearance and taste of whatever type of mushroom spore you used to animate it. The mushroom follows your instructions unless you tell it to do something stupid like jump off a cliff (it is intelligent after all).  The mushroom is friendly and stays animated for 1 hour then reverts back into a regular mushroom. You can cast Animated Shroom on it again to reanimate and it will retain its memories from before.
