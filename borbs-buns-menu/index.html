Borb’s Buns Menu¶

Hot Steam, Full Hearts, and Probably Too Much Filling¶

Baozi and Steamed Buns¶

Item Price Description
Classic Pork-ish Bao 4 sp Soft steamed bun filled with savory seasoned plant-meat, ginger, scallion, and sauce. Borb insists it is balanced. Marda insists it needs a catchier name.
Dockhand Breakfast Bao 5 sp Filled with scrambled egg-like tofu, peppered sausage crumble, potato, and onions. Designed to be eaten while walking, arguing, or already late.
Five-Spice Mushroom Bao 4 sp Earthy mushrooms, cabbage, soy glaze, and enough five-spice to make the bun smell smarter than you.
Chili Crisp Potato Bao 3 sp Mashed potato, chili crisp, scallions, and crispy bits. Cheap, filling, and beloved by people who own exactly one clean shirt.
Sweet Red Bean Cloud Bun 3 sp Soft, sweet, gentle, and one of the few items Borb admits is “pleasant.”
Maple Onion Breakfast Bun 5 sp Sweet onion, smoky breakfast crumble, maple glaze, and black pepper. Marda says it sells because people like being confused early.
The Argument Bao 6 sp Half spicy, half sweet, filled differently on each side. Couples order it and then immediately prove the name correct.
Borb’s Perfect Bao 8 sp Today’s best bun, according to Borb. He will explain why. You cannot stop him.
Nol’s Mystery Bun 2 sp Discount bun. Filling unknown. Nol promises it is “probably food.” Borb does not endorse this product.
Pip’s Quiet Bun 5 sp Mild-looking bun with a surprisingly deep, rich filling. Pip says, “It tastes like when people finally stop pretending.” No one knows what that means.

Meat Pies and Hand Pies¶

Item Price Description
Marda’s Proper Meat Pie 7 sp Thick-crusted, hearty, peppery, and clearly from Marda’s side of the family. Best eaten with both hands and no plans.
Railworker’s Pocket Pie 5 sp Portable savory pie filled with potato, gravy, and plant-meat. Fits in a coat pocket. Should not be kept there long.
Pepper-Lash Pie 8 sp Spicy gravy pie with black pepper, chili, and regret that arrives about ten seconds later.
The Apology Pie 6 sp Small heart-shaped pie Marda sells to people who need to say sorry but lack vocabulary. Comes with a blank note card.
Taxman’s Empty Pie 1 sp A joke item. It is just crust with air inside. Borb hates that it sells.
Grand Ring Special 9 sp Overpriced pie with too many layers and a very small filling. Marda says it is “satire.” Borb says satire should taste better.

Snacks and Sides¶

Item Price Description
Scallion Pancake Stack 3 sp Crispy, chewy, greasy in the correct way. Comes with dipping sauce unless Nol drank it.
Cucumber Smash Cup 2 sp Crushed cucumber, garlic, vinegar, chili oil, and sesame. Very refreshing, very loud-smelling.
Cabbage That Borb Respects 2 sp Steamed cabbage with soy, vinegar, and chili. Borb says cabbage deserves dignity.
Crispy Bun Ends 1 sp Fried scraps of dough tossed with salt. Marda calls it rustic. Tessa calls it inventory recovery.
Old Fen’s Table Pickles 1 sp Sharp, sour pickles from the jar Old Fen guards like a dragon. He says they are “for digestion and secrets.”
Soup of the Moment 3 sp Whatever soup Borb made to use things up. Usually good. Occasionally philosophical.

Sweets¶

Item Price Description
Sesame Sugar Bun 3 sp Sweet sesame paste in a soft bun. Leaves sugar on your fingers and peace in your heart.
Mooncake-ish Thing 4 sp Not technically a mooncake, according to three angry aunties. Still delicious.
Sticky Apricot Pocket 3 sp Fruit filling, soft dough, sticky edges. Children love it. Adults pretend they bought it for children.
Regretfully Large Cinnamon Twist 5 sp Huge, sweet, messy, and impossible to eat with dignity.

Drinks¶

Item Price Description
Hot Tea 1 sp Strong, simple, constantly refilled if Marda likes you.
Sweet Plum Tea 2 sp Tart, sweet, and excellent with spicy food.
Ginger Steam Cup 2 sp Hot ginger drink that clears the throat, sinuses, and possibly your past mistakes.
Borb’s Serious Broth 2 sp Savory sipping broth. Borb says it is not a drink. Marda sells it as one anyway.
Kid’s Fizzy Pear Cup 2 sp Lightly sparkling pear drink. Nol has been banned from shaking the bottles.

Daily Specials¶

d8 Special Price Description
1 The Debate Bun 6 sp Two fillings arguing inside one bun. Somehow both sides lose.
2 Mushroom Kingdom? Never Heard of It Bao 5 sp Mushroom bao with a deeply defensive name.
3 The Ringleader Roll 7 sp Circular bun with a dramatic swirl. Looks more important than it tastes.
4 Public Works Pocket Pie 5 sp Takes too long, costs more than expected, but is structurally sound.
5 Grandma Bunbury’s Emergency Bun 4 sp Plain, filling, comforting. For bad days and worse decisions.
6 Nol’s Volcano Bun 3 sp Spicy. Too spicy. Legally perhaps not a volcano.
7 Tessa’s Organized Lunch Box 8 sp One bao, one side, one sweet, one napkin, no chaos. Sells out immediately.
8 Pip’s Omen Dumplings 4 sp Six dumplings. One contains a tiny paper fortune. Pip says they are “not predictions, just warnings.”

Tiny Fortunes From Pip’s Omen Dumplings¶

d6 Fortune
1 “Do not trust a clean ledger.”
2 “Someone nearby is pretending not to listen.”
3 “Soup remembers.”
4 “Love is not strategy, but it helps.”
5 “If the bun is hollow, ask who took the filling.”
6 “You will soon need a napkin for reasons beyond food.”

Family Combo Deals¶

Combo Price Includes
Worker’s Lunch 8 sp Two bao, cucumber smash, hot tea.
Argument for Two 1 gp 4 sp Two Argument Bao, one shared side, two teas, and a napkin labeled “good luck.”
Borb’s Correct Meal 1 gp One Perfect Bao, one Serious Broth, one cabbage side. Borb nods approvingly if ordered.
Marda’s Crowd Pleaser 1 gp 5 sp Four mixed bao, one hand pie, crispy bun ends, and sweet plum tea.
Nol’s Bad Idea Box 7 sp One Mystery Bun, one Volcano Bun, fizzy pear cup, and a liability smile.
Fen’s Regular 6 sp Tea, pickles, one bun, and Old Fen’s unsolicited opinion.
