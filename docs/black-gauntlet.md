# Black Gauntlet — Rules Review

> **Status uncertain:** Krunk thinks Excalibur may have destroyed the Black Gauntlet. It is not listed as current equipment until that memory—and the final mechanics—are confirmed.

*Wondrous item, rare and evil; requires attunement*

Black metal ribbons clamp around the hand and forearm, terminating in a blood-red stone set into the palm. The gauntlet was built as an instrument of domination: it understands protection chiefly as preserving its owner long enough to hurt someone else.

## Legacy Protection — Preserved

These are the gauntlet's older rules, translated from the former character record:

- The wearer gains **+1 AC**.
- Against ranged attacks, the gauntlet instead provides **+2 AC**.
- The gauntlet may be used with a shield. While doing so, the wearer takes a **−2 penalty to Dexterity checks, Dexterity saving throws, and initiative** as the gauntlet locks the arm into a rigid defensive posture.

The old sheet recorded this as base AC 18 becoming **AC 19**, **AC 20 against ranged attacks**, or **AC 21 with a shield**, alongside “DEX −2.”

## Proposed Expanded Powers — Not Final

The gauntlet has **3 charges**, regains all charges at dusk, and uses **save DC 16**.

- **Ribbon Pulse — 1 charge, Action.** One creature within 30 feet makes a Strength save. On a failure, it takes `2d8` force damage and is pushed 15 feet directly away. On a success, it takes half damage and is not pushed.
- **Serpent Ward — 1 charge, Reaction.** When a ranged attack hits the wearer, the gauntlet adds **+3 AC against that attack only**, possibly causing it to miss. This does not stack with the gauntlet's ranged AC bonus; use whichever bonus is higher.
- **Neural Lash — 2 charges, Action.** One creature within 30 feet makes a Wisdom save. On a failure, it takes `3d6` psychic damage, cannot take reactions, and is frightened of the wearer until the end of the wearer's next turn. On a success, it takes half damage and suffers no other effect.
- **Tyrant's Touch — no charge, Action.** Manipulate one unattended object or unlocked mechanical control within 30 feet as though using *mage hand*. It cannot attack, pick locks, disarm traps, or lift more than 10 pounds.
- **Curse — System Lord's Pride.** Whenever a creature succeeds on a saving throw against the gauntlet, the wearer must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom save or become fixated on punishing that defiance. Until the end of the wearer's next turn, they have disadvantage on attacks against creatures other than that target. The gauntlet continually whispers that mercy is merely poor management.
