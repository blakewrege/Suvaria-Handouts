# The Pink Pony Inn

## Menu & House Guide

The Pink Pony is a bardcore tavern, cabaret, and inn where a spaghetti western collided with a gay furry revue and ordered another round. Rose lanterns, swinging doors, live music, curtained booths, and dramatic performers make it one of Armskirk's finest places to draw attention without drawing steel.

The house welcomes travelers, performers, queer locals, shifters, adventurers, and anyone prepared to respect the other guests, tip the talent, and make poor decisions entertainingly.

## Your Hosts

- **Madam Maeve Mane — Proprietor:** An impeccably dressed mare shifter and retired stage hostess. Maeve handles bookings, payroll, booth assignments, and the quiet removal of patrons who confuse hospitality with ownership.
- **Butch Glassidy — Head Bartender:** A broad bison shifter with polished horns, rolled sleeves, and the fastest pour in Armskirk's west end. Butch remembers every tab and can end an argument by serving the correct drink before anyone remembers ordering.
- **The House Band & Performers:** A rotating company of feline ribbon dancers, lupine stompers, avian aerialists, visiting musicians, and enthusiastic amateurs. They accept applause, coin, flowers, and useful gossip.

## Familiar Faces

- **Clint Wetwood:** An openly gay otter-shifter guide, owner of Wetwood's Brokeback Outfitters, enthusiastic Pink Pony regular, and full-time argument against subtlety. He is not technically one of the dancers. This distinction weakens as the evening continues.
- **Deep Snoat:** A discreet information broker associated with one of the curtained booths. Calling Snoat a gossip is like calling a crossbow a pointed suggestion. Appointments are not guaranteed, advertised, or improved by shouting.

## House Rules

1. No biting without consent.
2. No shedding on the stage.
3. Tip the performers.
4. The Dog Park is not discussed here.
5. If the Glow Cloud appears, finish your drink.
6. Draw attention freely; draw steel reluctantly.
7. If Clint says “duck,” react first and determine the species later.

## Tavern Effect Fine Print

Unless a duration is listed, a tavern effect lasts until used or until the end of the evening. Effects from multiple Pink Pony drinks do not stack; the newest drink replaces the previous drink's effect. The Pink Pony accepts no responsibility for strategic drinking, tactical glitter, or anyone who mistakes a bar tab for an adventuring build.

> **Butch's advice:** “If it glows, don't ask. If it moves, tip extra. If you start singing, you're doing it right.”
