Moon Gate Open-Air Theater¶

The Last Lantern at Eclipse Ford¶

Seven Farewells. One Sunrise. No Refunds for Crying.¶

Written by Mian dramatist Oru Pencraft twelve years after the War of Broken Chains, based upon interviews with the Jade Lantern Relief Company and adjusted wherever history failed to rhyme.

Tonight's Featured Players¶

  • Captain Suyin Vale: Mian combat surgeon, commander of the Jade Lantern field hospital, and enemy of preventable heroics.
  • Corporal Morel Button: Formerly enslaved Mycoterran sapper, bridge enthusiast, and enemy of bridges.
  • Lincohn Abraham: Young Leonian abolitionist, rail advocate, and owner of one axe that refuses to stay thrown.
  • Deuce Leem: Guardian spirit of Xuán Ji, master of water, and recent recipient of an extremely flexible entrance cue.
  • Major Jasper Hail: Commander of the New Moon Battalion, vampire cavalryman, and compelling argument for attending matinees.

The Three Acts¶

Act I — Pride and Field Dressing¶

Morel destroys a Confederate ammunition bridge several minutes before Suyin finishes using it. She repairs his shoulder while explaining the medical difference between courage and creating additional patients.

Act II — Seven Farewells¶

The New Moon Battalion closes upon Eclipse Ford. Suyin and Morel part seven times while Lincohn arrives axe-first to prevent the vampire officers from treating death as an extended intermission.

Act III — One Sunrise¶

The Jade Lanterns evacuate the wounded, Morel breaks the ford, Deuce answers the water drums, and Lincohn holds the riverbank until sunrise. Romance, abolition, flooding, and one entirely unauthorized hydration advertisement follow.

A Brief History for Patrons Who Skipped School¶

Twenty-five years ago, the land now called Mycoterra was the Confederated Cap States, ruled by vampire lords who enslaved people for their labor and blood. Enslaved Mycoterrans built Harriet Tubers' Underground Pipeway, spread Frederick Sporeglass's abolitionist papers, rose at Fort Shroomter, and formed the Army of the Free Caps under General Ulysses S. Grantoad.

The rebellion became the War of Broken Chains. Mian Lung sent the Jade Lantern Relief Company to carry medicine, engineers, and refugees through the fighting. Eclipse Ford was one of the war's final and wettest battles.

Slavery was wrong. The rebels were right. The vampires were somehow worse than their paperwork.

Why Everybody Is Arguing During Intermission¶

Mycoterra rebuilt for twenty years, then petitioned to become a Leonian province five years ago. Ringleader Lincohn vetoed admission after auditors found suspiciously uniform support and communities inspectors were forbidden to interview alone.

The later Open Ring Accord allows Mycoterra to sponsor Marshal Marioni's guest team in the Trials without granting provincial status. Supporters call it a fair path toward membership. Critics call it an unlocked window wearing a front-door hat.

Historical Accuracy Notice¶

Suyin Vale and Morel Button truly served at Eclipse Ford. Surviving letters prove they knew one another. Whether they were lovers remains unconfirmed, fiercely debated, and extremely profitable.

The management accepts no responsibility for soaked patrons, inaccurate spear grips, Glow Cloud conversions, or Lincohn speeches exceeding the advertised runtime.
