# Jade Steam Private Onsen

![Jade Steam Private Onsen during its peaceful daytime service](https://i.imgur.com/7i0OP7n.png)

*Reserved mineral baths, flowering courtyards, and staff professionally trained not to notice the next room.*

**Daylight reservations only. All guests must depart before sunset. Jade Steam does not provide overnight lodging, spirit arbitration, or name-recovery services.**

## Messages and Discreet Communication

Jade Steam will receive and send ordinary messages during daylight hours. Staff do not read sealed notes unless the note is on fire, leaking, screaming, or addressed to “whoever looks most trustworthy.”

| Service | Includes | Price |
|---|---|---:|
| **Front-Desk Message Hold** | Receive one note or small parcel for a named guest during their reservation | Free |
| **Local Runner Note** | Hand-delivered message within Xiaolong | 5 sp |
| **Armskirk Courier Note** | Sealed message delivered elsewhere in Armskirk | 1 gp |
| **Reply Card Service** | Staff leave a blank card, ink, and envelope in the suite and send the response afterward | 5 sp |
| **Do Not Disturb Seal** | Staff intercept routine visitors and messages until the guest checks out | 5 sp |
| **Priority Summons** | Urgent runner service for physicians, legal counsel, household staff, or someone the guest swears is “not a problem, just complicated” | 2 gp |

**Privacy note:** Jade Steam confirms whether a message was accepted, not whether a guest is present. The staff did not notice who entered the next room and are not beginning with your courier.

## House Rules

1. Wash before entering the pools.
2. Weapons remain in the cedar lockers. Heroic destinies may be checked at the desk.
3. The staff did not notice the next room. Neither did you.
4. Cannonballs are billed according to displaced water.
5. If the tenth bell rings during your appointment, please proceed calmly to an exit that still exists.
