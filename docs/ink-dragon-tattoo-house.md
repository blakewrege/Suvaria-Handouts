# Ink Dragon Tattoo House

*Pain is included. Regret is billed separately.*

**Consultation:** 10 gp, credited toward any commissioned tattoo. Every symbol is translated before application. Every magical effect—and every known drawback—is written down before payment. No permanent magical work is performed on an intoxicated client, regardless of how persuasive his emergency sake may be.

**Prices are negotiable.** Zhen accepts reasonable offers, useful barter, rare pigments, documented field testing, and interest-free installments. She will reduce the price before she reduces the safety.

### Dragon Sneeze

As an action, scratch one of the dragon's four seasoning runes. The ink dragon races up your body, braces its tiny claws across your ribs, and magically overinflates your lungs. You then unleash a sneeze in a **30-foot cone**.

**Dragon Sneeze save DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier.** This design is an exception to the shop's usual mental-ability tattoo DC: the dragon supplies the element, but your muscles supply the launch.

Each creature in the cone makes the rune's listed saving throw. On a failure, it takes **6d8 damage** and suffers the listed rider. On a success, it takes half damage and suffers no rider.

| Seasoning Rune | Target's Save | Damage | Failed-Save Rider and Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ember Chili** | Dexterity | `6d8` fire | The creature catches fire for **up to 1 minute**. At the start of each of its turns, it takes `1d8` fire damage. The burning ends when the creature or someone within 5 feet uses an action to extinguish it, when it is thoroughly doused, or when magic extinguishes the flames. Unattended flammable objects in the cone ignite. |
| **Wintermint** | Constitution | `6d8` cold | Ice locks the creature in place, reducing its speed to **0 until the end of its next turn**. On that turn, it may use an action to make a Strength (Athletics) check against the Dragon Sneeze DC, ending the effect early on a success. |
| **Storm Salt** | Dexterity | `6d8` lightning | The creature convulses with blue sparks and **cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn**. Any reaction it had already spent remains spent; the tattoo is powerful, not an accountant. |
| **Black Pepper** | Strength | `6d8` thunder | A Large or smaller creature is pushed **20 feet directly away** and knocked prone. A Huge creature is pushed **10 feet** but is not knocked prone. A Gargantuan creature is not moved. The movement is instantaneous; a prone creature remains prone until it stands normally. |

The sneeze instantly disperses mundane smoke, fog, and gas in the cone and flings unsecured Tiny objects `1d4 × 10` feet away without dealing damage. It is audible from **300 feet** away. Everyone in the cone smells like the chosen seasoning for **1 hour**, including creatures that succeeded on the save; Zhen insists this is how you know it worked.

## Magical-Ink Rules

- A wearer may safely maintain permanent magical tattoos equal to their **proficiency bonus**.
- A wearer may also carry active Flash Ink designs equal to their **proficiency bonus**. Extra Flash Ink remains inert until another design is spent or fades.
- Only one magical tattoo may occupy each arm, leg, torso side, back, or head and neck.
- Multiple copies of the same magical design do not grant additional uses or stack their effects.
- Temporary ink does not count against capacity but cannot overlap another active magical design.
- Magical tattoos do **not** increase Armor Class. If you want armor, Zhen knows several people who sell armor.
- Ordinary injuries do not harm magical ink. Catastrophic wounds, dropping to 0 hit points from damage across the design, or deliberate mutilation may cause it to fade or go dormant.
- Dormant or damaged tattoos can usually be repaired. Complete destruction of the tattooed skin may destroy the magic.
