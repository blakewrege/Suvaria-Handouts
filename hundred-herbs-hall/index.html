Hundred Herbs Hall¶

Tonics, Oils, Powders, and Professional Concern¶

Tell us what bit you before it becomes relevant to the next customer.

Prices are negotiable. Empty bottles, useful monster ingredients, field reports, and sensible favors are accepted. Instructions are included at no additional charge and ignored at considerable additional charge.

Household and Road Remedies¶

Preparation Use Price
Morning-After Mercy Suppresses an ordinary hangover for 4 hours. It does not restore memories, dignity, or money. 5 sp
Road-Rash Salve Enough for one treatment of blisters, rope burn, saddle rub, or armor chafing. 4 sp
Monster-Rash Paste Grants advantage on one save or Medicine check against a nonmagical rash, itch, or skin irritant. 8 sp
Quiet Bones Tea Helps one willing creature sleep through ordinary street and tavern noise for 4 hours. Alarms, attacks, and bards with percussion still wake them. 5 sp
Emberleaf Burn Salve Grants advantage on one Medicine check made to treat a recent mundane burn. 1 gp
Red March Liniment After a short rest, ignore ordinary muscle soreness and forced-march aches for 8 hours. 1 gp

Combat Tonics¶

One combat tonic per short rest is safe. Additional doses require increasingly difficult Constitution saves; failure leaves the drinker poisoned for 1 hour even though the tonic still works.

Tonic Effect Price
Cat's Borrowed Eyes Gain darkvision to 60 feet for 1 hour, or extend existing darkvision by 30 feet to a maximum of 120 feet. Bright light imposes disadvantage on sight-based Perception checks for the duration. 6 gp
Swallowtail Draft For the next 3 rounds, regain 1 hit point at the start of each turn while you have at least 1 hit point. It cannot revive an unconscious creature. 8 gp
Stone-Lung Syrup For 1 hour, gain advantage on saves against inhaled gas, smoke, and spores. Your voice becomes a dramatic gravelly whisper. 10 gp
Black Gull Focus For 1 hour, after failing one Constitution save to maintain concentration, add 1d4 to the roll. The tonic then ends. 12 gp
Field-Stitch Cordial During your next short rest, reroll any 1s on Hit Dice spent for healing. Use each new roll. 12 gp
Graveflower Reserve For 1 hour, the first time you fall to 0 hit points without dying outright, you automatically become stable. The tonic then ends. 20 gp
Thunderroot As a bonus action, gain 10 feet of speed and advantage on Strength checks for 1 minute. Afterward, you cannot Dash until you complete a short rest. 25 gp

Monster Oils¶

Applying an oil takes 1 minute. One weapon can carry only one oil, lasting 1 hour or until its listed hits are used.

Oil Effect Price
Moon-Silver Polish The weapon counts as silvered for 1 hour. It does not become magical. 8 gp
Grave Grease The next three hits against undead deal an extra 1d4 radiant damage. 12 gp
Rotbane Resin The next three hits against plants or fungi deal an extra 1d4 fire damage. The weapon does not ignite unattended scenery. 12 gp
Chainbreaker Oil The next three hits against a creature currently grappling or restraining someone deal an extra 1d4 damage of the weapon's type. 15 gp
Wraithsalt Lacquer The next hit against an incorporeal creature deals an extra 2d6 radiant damage, then the oil ends. 20 gp

Powders and Throwing Packets¶

Throwing a packet uses an action, has a range of 20 feet, and normally uses save DC 13.

Packet Effect Price
Puffcap Pouch Creates a heavily obscured 10-foot-radius cloud until the start of your next turn. Wind may disperse it early. 4 gp
Peacebond Pollen Creatures in a 10-foot-radius cloud make a Constitution save or cannot take reactions until the start of your next turn. 6 gp
Devil's Lantern Dust Invisible creatures in a 10-foot-radius burst make a Dexterity save or become visibly outlined until the end of your next turn. 8 gp
Specter Salt Cover one 5-foot square until the start of your next turn. Incorporeal creatures cannot pass through the covered floor or wall. 10 gp
Moon Dust Packet Make a ranged attack using Dexterity and proficiency against one creature. On a hit, it cannot become invisible or change shape until the end of your next turn. 15 gp

Proper Medicine¶

Item Effect Price
Standard Healing Potion Restores 2d4 + 2 hit points. Usually only two are ready each day. 35 gp
White Honey Purge Gain advantage on your next save against an ingested poison within 1 hour. It does not erase the consequences of drinking several combat tonics. 5 gp
Poison or venom identification Bring the substance, container, affected weapon, or something it recently killed. 2–5 gp
Custom monster preparation Requires a useful sample and a consultation. Please label which end of the monster it came from. From 25 gp

Natural does not mean safe. Bears are natural.
