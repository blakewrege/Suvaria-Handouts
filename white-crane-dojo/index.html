White Crane Dojo¶

  • Mian name: Baihe Wuguan
  • Location: Xiaolong district, Armskirk
  • Known for: Martial arts, public demonstrations, youth lessons, community defense, and educational soreness

The White Crane Dojo teaches crane forms, tiger forms, flowing-river forms, and the ancient beginner's discipline of discovering how flexible you are not. Its whitewashed courtyards and rain-dark practice stones are open to respectful visitors for morning demonstrations, supervised lessons, and tea afterward.

The headmaster is Master Shrew Fu, a tiny elderly shrew shifter whose patience is shorter than he is and whose walking staff has never once been used for walking. He teaches timing, leverage, and the comforting principle that a larger opponent is merely a lever with poor judgment.

Senior instructor Beakatrice Plume, a red-crowned crane shifter, teaches the dojo's graceful White Crane forms. She can correct a student's posture with one tap of her folding fan and a disappointed silence audible from across the courtyard.

Conditioning instructor Jean-Clawed Van Ram teaches high kicks, practical defense, and dramatic split stances. His horns require him to enter several classrooms sideways; he considers this an architectural weakness.

Visitors may observe quietly, book a beginner lesson, or ache educationally. Advanced training is also available by appointment and by signing a document acknowledging that “advanced” is not a synonym for “comfortable.”
