## Maul of Maelstroms (Caracus)

*Maul, uncommon (requires attunement)*
___
You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic  weapon. The bonus damage is lightning damage. Once per long rest you can shout a battle cry and slam an enemy dealing an extra 1d10 thunder damage. They must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked back 10ft and prone.
You feel a slight shock whenever you pick it up, the weapon seems to vibrate with energy

## Ring of the Gladiators (Krunk)

___
Explosive applause every time you crit to attack or deliver a killing blow to an enemy. Add 1d6 to your next attack roll.
 Laugh track if the wielder crit-fails on an attack roll. Subtract 1d6 to your next attack roll.

## Ring of Charm (Bells)
*Wondrous item, uncommon*
___
3 charges per day. You may cast [Charm Person](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Charm%20Person#content) (DC 15) but you must shake the targets hand(with your ring hand) in order to do so. The target does not learn you tried to charm it on the first usage regardless of success or fail.

## Eyes of the Eagle (Ella)
*Wondrous item, uncommon*
___
While wearing them, you have advantage on Perception and Investigation checks that rely on sight. In conditions of clear visibility, you can make out details of even extremely distant creatures and objects as small as 2 feet across.

## Boots of Elvenkind  (Vex)
*Wondrous item, uncommon*
___
While you wear these boots, your steps make no sound, regardless of the surface you are moving across. You also have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks that rely on moving silently.

## Veil of Shadows (Krunk)
*Wondrous item, rare*
___
 A large black amulet also known as Shield of the Void. Once per day you can use a Bonus Action or a Reaction to speak this amulet's Command Word to create an aura that lightly obscures and gives resistance to all damage types until your next turn to everything in a 10 ft radius around you.

## Sword of Screams (Krunk)
*Longsword, uncommon*
___
When pulled from it’s scabbard everyone within 60ft of it is deafened for 1d4 turns.  It has the same stats as a Longsword and is magical. While weilding this weapon you may ask DM the to play selected screamo or metal music.

## Talking Skull (Bells)
*Wondrous item, uncommon*
___
This skull has forgotten its name and seems to be constantly eyeing you.  Once per day you can animate the skull, lighting up its eyes with a pale blue light. Roll a d6 and a d100. While animated it speaks with you and will answer a question to the best of its ability if you correctly guess a riddle.

## Garland of Garlic (Caracas)
*Net, uncommon*
___
A magical garland of garlic laced with silver barbs. On hit, it wraps around the vampirie's neck and prevents it from using its bite action. Cannot be removed or destroyed by a vampire regardless of shape changing. Has the same properties as a [Net](https://www.dndbeyond.com/equipment/net)

## Bone Breaker (Caracus)

*Mace, uncommon (requires attunement)*
___
A large spiked mace that says “Bone Breaker, do not use!” hastily written across it.
1d6 bludgeoning, +1 piercing, +1 to hit, is Magical
- **Break Bones (Recharge 5-6):** Melee Attack, on hit the target must succeed a Constitution Save(DC 14) or have their AC reduced by 1 and speed reduced by 10ft until healed.
- **Hunger for Blood:** At the start of combat the wielder must make a Wisdom Save(DC 14). On fail, you must dash and Reckless Attack(Barbarian) an enemy within melee range of Bone Breaker.
- **Bloodied Rage:** While the wielder is bloodied in combat this weapon deals an extra 1d4 bludgeoning damage. At the start of his turn the weilder must make a Constitution Save(DC 14) to avoid attacking the nearest creature (random if more than one adjacent).

## Chilling Halberd (Krunk)
*Halberd, uncommon (requires attunement)*
___
You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon and can apply the Call of the Grave affect when you hit.
- **Call of the Grave (Recharge 6):** Add 1d6 Necrotic Damage, a ghostly skeletal hand attaches to the target and they can’t regain hit points until the start of your next turn. Until then, the hand clings to the target. If you hit an undead target, it also has disadvantage on its next attack roll against you until the start of your next turn.

## Shadow Dagger (SOLD)
*Dagger, rare (requires attunement)*
___
You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.
- **Returning:** Shadow Dagger returns to the wielder’s hand immediately after it is used to make a ranged attack.
- **Backstabber:** Roll an addtional 1d4 damage when the target is flatfooted.
- **Create Undead Minion (Long Rest):** Once per long rest you may spend an action to stab the Shadow Dagger into a fresh corpse. The corpse becomes an undead minion and obeys your commands until it is destroyed or you use this ability again. (high CR creatures will be reduced to one)

## Demon Mask (SOLD)

## Necklace of lightning bolts  (Strawberry)
*Wondrous Guisarme, uncommon*
___
Once per turn, you can use an action to detach a bead. When you do a 3rd-level Lightning Bolt spell (save DC 14) is created in the direction of your choice.

You can detach multiple beads, or even the whole necklace, as one action. When you do so, increase the level of the Lightning Bolt by 1 for each bead beyond the first. You may use this while wildshaped if the creature has enough manual dexterity.

## Kinesis Module (Ella)

*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*

- **Kinesis Grab:** As an action, you can cast *Mage Hand*. The spectral hand created by this spell is invisible.

- **Kinesis Launch (Recharge after a Short Rest):** Once per short rest, you can cast *Catapult* at the 4th level without using a spell slot (6d8). The damage dealt by this ability can be modified by the item you choose to throw.

The Kinesis Module, inspired by Dead Space, grants the wielder telekinetic abilities. This magical item is perfect for tactically minded characters who wish to manipulate their environment and use objects as weapons in creative ways.
